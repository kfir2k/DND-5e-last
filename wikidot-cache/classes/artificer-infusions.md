# Artificer Infusions

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/artificer:infusions
Fetched: 2026-11-17 (HTTP 200, page read in full, ~18,300 characters)

> **Paraphrased, not verbatim** — the fetch tool won't reproduce the page word for word. Full coverage of
> every infusion, prerequisite, item line and table row; wording is paraphrase.

Intro: an infusion turns a nonmagical object into a magic item; each entry names the eligible object and
whether attunement is needed; some have a minimum artificer level; unless stated otherwise, an infusion
can't be learned more than once. 17 infusions on the page; the only one marked **(UA)** is Armor of Tools.

1. **Arcane Propulsion Armor** — prereq 14th level. Item: a suit of armor (attunement). +5 ft. walking speed;
   the gauntlets are magic melee weapons dealing 1d8 force, returning to the wearer after a thrown attack;
   the armor can't be removed against the wearer's will and replaces missing limbs.
2. **Armor of Magical Strength** — Item: a suit of armor (attunement). 6 charges; spend one to add INT mod
   to a STR check or save, or as a reaction to avoid being knocked prone. Regains 1d6 at dawn.
3. **Armor of Tools (UA)** — Item: a suit of armor. Action: integrate artisan's or thieves' tools for 8 hours
   (one at a time); add INT mod to checks with them. *UA — not used in the app.*
4. **Boots of the Winding Path** — prereq 6th. Item: boots (attunement). Bonus action: teleport up to 15 ft.
   to an unoccupied space you can see that you occupied earlier this turn.
5. **Enhanced Arcane Focus** — Item: rod, staff or wand (attunement). +1 to spell attack rolls and ignore
   half cover on spell attacks; +2 at 10th level.
6. **Enhanced Defense** — Item: armor or a shield. +1 AC; +2 at 10th level.
7. **Enhanced Weapon** — Item: a simple or martial weapon. +1 to attack and damage; +2 at 10th level.
8. **Helm of Awareness** — prereq 10th. Item: a helmet (attunement). Advantage on initiative; can't be
   surprised unless incapacitated.
9. **Homunculus Servant** — Item: a gem or crystal worth at least 100 gp. Creates a friendly Tiny construct
   homunculus (stats scale with your PB and artificer level): AC 13, speed 20 ft., fly 30 ft.; STR 4, DEX 15,
   CON 12, INT 10, WIS 10, CHA 7; Force Strike (1d4 + PB force); Evasion; Channel Magic reaction. Shares your
   initiative, takes the Dodge action unless commanded; mending restores 2d6 HP; vanishes if you or it dies.
10. **Mind Sharpener** — Item: armor or robes. 4 charges; reaction when the wearer fails a CON save to keep
    concentration: spend one to succeed instead. Regains 1d4 at dawn.
11. **Radiant Weapon** — prereq 6th. Item: a simple or martial weapon (attunement). +1 attack and damage;
    bonus action to shed bright light 30 ft. and dim light 30 ft. more. 4 charges: reaction when hit by an
    attack, the attacker is blinded until the end of its next turn unless it succeeds on a CON save against
    your spell save DC. Regains 1d4 at dawn.
12. **Repeating Shot** — Item: a simple or martial weapon with the ammunition property (attunement). +1 to
    ranged attack and damage; ignores loading; makes its own magic ammunition if none is loaded (vanishes
    after the attack).
13. **Replicate Magic Item** — replicate one item from the tables below or a common magic item from the DMG
    (no potions or scrolls; Xanathar's common items allowed). Can be learned multiple times.
    - 2nd: Alchemy Jug, Bag of Holding, Cap of Water Breathing, Goggles of Night, Rope of Climbing, Sending
      Stones, Wand of Magic Detection, Wand of Secrets (none require attunement)
    - 6th: Boots of Elvenkind, Cloak of Elvenkind (att.), Cloak of the Manta Ray, Eyes of Charming (att.),
      Gloves of Thievery, Lantern of Revealing, Pipes of Haunting, Ring of Water Walking
    - 10th (all attunement except Quiver of Ehlonna): Boots of Striding and Springing, Boots of the
      Winterlands, Bracers of Archery, Brooch of Shielding, Cloak of Protection, Eyes of the Eagle, Gauntlets
      of Ogre Power, Gloves of Missile Snaring, Gloves of Swimming and Climbing, Hat of Disguise, Headband of
      Intellect, Helm of Telepathy, Medallion of Thoughts, Necklace of Adaptation, Periapt of Wound Closure,
      Pipes of the Sewers, Quiver of Ehlonna, Ring of Jumping, Ring of Mind Shielding, Slippers of Spider
      Climbing, Ventilating Lungs (Eberron), Winged Boots
    - 14th (attunement except Dimensional Shackles and Horn of Blasting): Amulet of Health, Arcane Propulsion
      Arm (Eberron), Belt of Hill Giant Strength, Boots of Levitation, Boots of Speed, Bracers of Defense,
      Cloak of the Bat, Dimensional Shackles, Gem of Seeing, Horn of Blasting, Ring of Free Action, Ring of
      Protection, Ring of the Ram
14. **Repulsion Shield** — prereq 6th. Item: a shield (attunement). +1 AC; 4 charges: reaction when hit by a
    melee attack, push the attacker up to 15 ft. away. Regains 1d4 at dawn.
15. **Resistant Armor** — prereq 6th. Item: armor (attunement). Resistance to one damage type chosen when
    infused: acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, or thunder.
16. **Returning Weapon** — Item: a simple or martial weapon with the thrown property. +1 attack and damage;
    returns to the wielder's hand right after it's used for a ranged attack.
17. **Spell-Refueling Ring** — prereq 6th. Item: a ring (attunement). Action: recover one expended spell slot
    of 3rd level or lower; then not again until the next dawn.
