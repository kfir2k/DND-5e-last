## Hit points, proficiencies, equipment

- Hit Dice 1d8 per artificer level; 8 + CON mod at 1st level; then 1d8 (or 5) + CON mod per level.
- Armor: light and medium armor, shields. Weapons: simple weapons.
- Tools: thieves' tools, tinker's tools, one type of artisan's tools of your choice.
- Saving throws: Constitution, Intelligence.
- Skills: choose two from Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Sleight of Hand.
- Equipment: any two simple weapons; a light crossbow and 20 bolts; studded leather armor or scale mail;
  thieves' tools and a dungeoneer's pack (plus background equipment) — or 5d4 × 10 gp instead.
- Multiclassing: Intelligence 13 or higher to multiclass in or out (the page's exact sentence:
  "You must have an Intelligence score of 13 or higher in order to multiclass in or out of this class.").

## Features

- **Magical Tinkering (1st):** action, holding thieves' tools or artisan's tools, touch a Tiny nonmagical
  object and give it one property: it sheds light; it records a message (up to 6 seconds) that plays when
  tapped; it gives off a continuous odor or nonverbal sound; or it shows a static visual effect on a
  surface. Lasts indefinitely; end it as an action. Objects at once = INT mod (minimum one).
- **Spellcasting (1st):**
  - Tools Required: thieves' tools or an artisan's tool you're proficient with is the focus for spells with
    a material component (and you must have one in hand to cast). From 2nd level, an item bearing one of your
    infusions works as a focus too.
  - Cantrips: two at 1st level, more per the table; swap one when you gain a level.
  - Preparing and casting: prepare INT mod + half artificer level (rounded down, minimum one) from the
    artificer list after a long rest; slots return on a long rest.
  - Spellcasting ability: Intelligence. Spell save DC "8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence
    modifier"; spell attack modifier "your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier" (exact).
  - Ritual casting: prepared artificer spells with the ritual tag.
  - Spellcasting focus: covered under Tools Required.
- **Infuse Item (2nd):** infusions known per the table (4 at 2nd); swap one on gaining a level. At the end of
  a long rest, touch nonmagical objects to turn them into magic items, up to the Infused Items count. Each
  infusion lasts until replaced; if you die, they end after INT mod days (minimum one).
- **Artificer Specialist (3rd):** choose a specialty; features again at 5th, 9th, 15th.
- **The Right Tool for the Job (3rd):** with thieves' or artisan's tools in hand, 1 hour of work creates one
  set of nonmagical artisan's tools in an unoccupied space within 5 ft.
- **Ability Score Improvement:** 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, 19th.
- **Tool Expertise (6th):** proficiency bonus doubled for any ability check using a tool proficiency.
- **Flash of Genius (7th):** reaction when you or a creature you can see within 30 ft. makes an ability
  check or saving throw: add your INT mod to the roll. Uses = INT mod (minimum one), regained on a long rest.
- **Magic Item Adept (10th):** attune to up to four magic items; crafting a common or uncommon magic item
  takes a quarter of the normal time and half the gold.
- **Spell-Storing Item (11th):** after a long rest, store a 1st- or 2nd-level artificer spell with a casting
  time of 1 action in a simple or martial weapon or a spellcasting focus. A creature holding it can use an
  action to produce the spell's effect. Uses = twice INT mod (minimum two), until a new spell is stored.
- **Magic Item Savant (14th):** attune to up to five magic items; ignore class, race, spell and level
  requirements on attuning to or using a magic item.
- **Magic Item Master (18th):** attune to up to six magic items.
- **Soul of Artifice (20th):** +1 to all saving throws per magic item you're attuned to; when reduced to
  0 HP but not killed outright, reaction to end one of your infusions and drop to 1 HP instead.
