## 3rd Level - Dreadful Strikes

When you hit a creature with a weapon, you can deal an extra 1d4 psychic damage to the target, which can take this extra damage only once per turn. This increases to 1d6 at 11th level.

## 3rd Level - Fey Wanderer Magic

Bonus spells at specific levels: Charm Person (3rd), Misty Step (5th), Dispel Magic (9th), Dimension Door (13th), and Mislead (17th). These count as ranger spells for you, but don't count against the number of ranger spells you know.

## 3rd Level - Otherworldly Glamour

Whenever you make a Charisma check, you gain a bonus to the check equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of +1). Additionally, gain proficiency in one skill from: Deception, Performance, or Persuasion.

## 7th Level - Beguiling Twist

Advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened. When nearby creatures succeed against charm/frightened effects, use a reaction to force a different creature within 120 feet to make a Wisdom save or become charmed/frightened by you for 1 minute, repeatable each turn.

## 11th Level - Fey Reinforcements

Learn Summon Fey without using a spell slot once per long rest. Whenever you start casting the spell, you can modify it so that it doesn't require concentration, reducing duration to 1 minute.

## 15th Level - Misty Wanderer

Cast Misty Step without expending spell slots a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum once) per long rest. Can bring one willing creature within 5 feet along to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of destination.
