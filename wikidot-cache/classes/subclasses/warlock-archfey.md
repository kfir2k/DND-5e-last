## Class Features

**Fey Presence (Level 1)**
"As an action, you can cause each creature in a 10-foot cube originating from you to make a Wisdom
saving throw against your warlock spell save DC." Creatures that fail are charmed or frightened
(your choice) until the end of your next turn. Once per short or long rest.

**Misty Escape (Level 6)**
Reaction when you take damage: turn invisible and teleport up to 60 ft. to an unoccupied space you
can see. Invisibility lasts until the start of your next turn or until you attack or cast a spell.
Once per short or long rest.

**Beguiling Defenses (Level 10)**
Immune to being charmed. When another creature attempts to charm you, reaction to turn the charm
back on it: it makes a Wisdom save against your spell save DC or is charmed by you for 1 minute (or
until it takes damage).

**Dark Delirium (Level 14)**
Action: target a creature within 60 ft. — Wisdom save against your spell save DC or it's charmed or
frightened by you for 1 minute (or until your concentration breaks); while affected it perceives
only you, itself, and an illusory realm around it. Once per short or long rest.
