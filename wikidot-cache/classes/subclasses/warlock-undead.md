# The Undead (Warlock subclass)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/warlock:undead
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Expanded Spell List

1st: Bane, False Life. 2nd: Blindness/Deafness, Phantasmal Force. 3rd: Phantom Steed, Speak with
Dead. 4th: Death Ward, Greater Invisibility. 5th: Antilife Shell, Cloudkill.

## Class Features

**Form of Dread (Level 1)**
Bonus action: transform into a dreadful visage for 1 minute (or until you end it as a bonus
action). While transformed: gain temporary HP equal to 1d10 + your warlock level; once per turn on
a hit, force the target to make a Wisdom save against your spell save DC or be frightened of you
until the end of your next turn; and you're immune to being frightened. Uses equal to your
proficiency bonus, regained on a long rest.

**Grave Touched (Level 6)**
You don't need to eat, drink, or breathe. Once per turn when you deal damage with a warlock spell
or Eldritch Blast, you can change its damage type to necrotic. While using Form of Dread, add an
extra damage die to one damage roll you make.

**Necrotic Husk (Level 10)**
Resistance to necrotic damage (immunity while transformed by Form of Dread). When reduced to 0 HP
but not killed outright, you can use your reaction to drop to 1 HP instead; when you do, each
creature of your choice within 30 ft. takes 2d10 + your warlock level necrotic damage, and you
gain one level of exhaustion. Usable again only after finishing a number of long rests (1d4).

**Spirit Projection (Level 14)**
Action: project your spirit out of your body (which is left behind, incapacitated) for up to 1 hour
or until your concentration breaks. While projected: resistance to necrotic, poison, bludgeoning,
piercing, and slashing damage; no verbal/somatic/material components needed for necromancy and
conjuration spells you cast; flying speed equal to your walking speed; and healing you receive from
necrotic-damage sources is halved. Once per long rest.
