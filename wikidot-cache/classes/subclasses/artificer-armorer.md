# Artificer: Armorer

Fetched: 2026-11-17 (HTTP 200, read in full; no (UA) markers — the separate UA Armorer is a different page)
Book: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

> **Paraphrased, not verbatim** (fetch tool quote limit). Full coverage of every feature and number.

- **Tools of the Trade (3rd):** proficiency with heavy armor and smith's tools (if already proficient with
  smith's tools, one other artisan's tool).
- **Armorer Spells (3rd):** always prepared, don't count against prepared spells. 3rd Magic Missile,
  Thunderwave · 5th Mirror Image, Shatter · 9th Hypnotic Pattern, Lightning Bolt · 13th Fire Shield, Greater
  Invisibility · 17th Passwall, Wall of Force.
- **Arcane Armor (3rd):** action, smith's tools in hand: turn armor you wear into Arcane Armor. Ignore its
  Strength requirement; it's a spellcasting focus for artificer spells; it attaches to you, can't be removed
  against your will, covers your whole body, replaces missing limbs; deploy or retract the helmet as a bonus
  action; don or doff it as an action. Stays Arcane Armor until you don another suit or die.
- **Armor Model (3rd):** Guardian or Infiltrator; the model's special weapon uses INT instead of STR/DEX for
  attack and damage rolls. Change model after a short or long rest with smith's tools in hand.
  - Guardian — Thunder Gauntlets: each counts as a simple melee weapon while you hold nothing in it, 1d8
    thunder on a hit; a creature hit has disadvantage on attack rolls against targets other than you until the
    start of your next turn. Defensive Field: bonus action, gain temp HP equal to your artificer level
    (replacing any you have), lost if you doff the armor; uses = proficiency bonus per long rest.
  - Infiltrator — Lightning Launcher: gemlike node on a fist or chest; simple ranged weapon, range 90/300,
    1d6 lightning; once per turn on a hit, extra 1d6 lightning to that target. Powered Steps: +5 ft. walking
    speed. Dampening Field: advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks; cancels out armor-imposed disadvantage.
- **Extra Attack (5th):** attack twice when you take the Attack action.
- **Armor Modifications (9th):** for Infuse Item, the Arcane Armor counts as separate items — armor (chest
  piece), boots, helmet, special weapon; each can bear one infusion, which transfers when you change model.
  Max infused items +2, but the extra items must be part of the Arcane Armor.
- **Perfected Armor (15th):**
  - Guardian: reaction when a Huge or smaller creature you can see ends its turn within 30 ft.: it makes a STR
    save against your spell save DC or is pulled up to 25 ft. to an unoccupied space; if it ends within 5 ft. of
    you, make a melee weapon attack against it as part of the reaction. Uses = proficiency bonus per long rest.
  - Infiltrator: a creature that takes lightning damage from the Lightning Launcher glimmers until the start of
    your next turn (dim light, 5-ft. radius); it has disadvantage on attacks against you, and the next attack roll
    against it has advantage and deals an extra 1d6 lightning on a hit.
