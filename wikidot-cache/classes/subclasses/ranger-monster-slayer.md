## 3rd Level - Monster Slayer Magic

Bonus spells at specific levels: Protection from Evil and Good (3rd), Zone of Truth (5th), Magic Circle (9th), Banishment (13th), Hold Monster (17th).

## 3rd Level - Hunter's Sense

Action: choose one creature you can see within 60 feet of you and immediately learn whether it has any damage immunities, resistances, or vulnerabilities. Uses equal to Wisdom modifier (minimum once), regained on a long rest.

## 3rd Level - Slayer's Prey

Bonus action: designate one creature you can see within 60 feet of you. The first time each turn you hit that target with a weapon attack, it takes an extra 1d6 damage. Lasts until short/long rest, or until you designate a new target.

## 7th Level - Supernatural Defense

Whenever the target of your Slayer's Prey forces you to make a saving throw, or you make an ability check to escape that target's grapple, add 1d6 to your roll.

## 11th Level - Magic-User's Nemesis

Reaction when you see a creature casting a spell or teleporting within 60 feet of you: the creature must succeed on a Wisdom save against your spell save DC or its spell/teleport fails. Usable once per short or long rest.

## 15th Level - Slayer's Counter

If the target of your Slayer's Prey forces you to make a saving throw, use your reaction to make one weapon attack against it immediately before the save. If the attack hits, your save automatically succeeds.
