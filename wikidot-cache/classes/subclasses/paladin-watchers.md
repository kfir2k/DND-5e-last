# Oath of the Watchers (Paladin)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/paladin:watchers (colon replaced with a dash in filename per this folder's README)
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Oath Spells
- 3rd: Alarm, Detect Magic
- 5th: Moonbeam, See Invisibility
- 9th: Counterspell, Nondetection
- 13th: Aura of Purity, Banishment
- 17th: Hold Monster, Scrying

## Channel Divinity Options (3rd level)
**Watcher's Will:** Channel Divinity — choose a number of creatures you can see within 30 feet, up
to a number equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum one); for 1 minute you and they have
advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws.

**Abjure the Extraplanar:** Action, Channel Divinity — each aberration, celestial, elemental, fey,
or fiend within 30 feet that can see/hear you makes a Wisdom save or is turned for 1 minute or
until it takes damage; a turned creature must spend its turns trying to move as far from you as it
can and can't willingly end its move within 30 feet of you.

## Aura of the Sentinel (7th level)
You and creatures of your choice within 10 feet of you (30 feet at 18th level) gain a bonus to
initiative rolls equal to your proficiency bonus.

## Vigilant Rebuke (15th level)
Reaction: whenever you or a creature you can see within 30 feet succeeds on an Intelligence,
Wisdom, or Charisma saving throw, deal 2d8 + your Charisma modifier force damage to the creature
that forced that save. No stated per-rest limit.

## Mortal Bulwark (20th level)
Bonus action: transform for 1 minute, gaining truesight out to 120 feet and advantage on attack
rolls against aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, and fiends; you can also force one such
creature you can see within 120 feet to make a Charisma save or be banished to its home plane
(if native to the current plane, it's frightened instead). Recharges on a long rest or by
expending a 5th-level spell slot.
