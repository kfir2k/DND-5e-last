# Clockwork Soul (Sorcerer)

Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Class Features

**Clockwork Magic (1st Level)**
Learn bonus spells at specific levels (1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th). Each counts as a sorcerer spell but
doesn't count against spells known. You can swap a gained spell for an abjuration/transmutation
spell from the sorcerer, warlock, or wizard list on a later level-up. Additionally, choose or
randomly determine a cosmetic way your connection to order manifests while casting your sorcerer
spells (a Manifestations table with 6 cosmetic options, e.g. cogwheels or glowing skin).

**Restore Balance (1st Level)**
When a creature you can see within 60 feet of you is about to roll a d20 with advantage or
disadvantage, you can use your reaction to prevent the roll from being affected by advantage and
disadvantage. Uses equal to proficiency bonus per long rest.

**Bastion of Law (6th Level)**
Expend 1-5 sorcery points to create a ward on yourself or a creature within 30 feet as an action.
The ward lasts until a long rest or you create a new one. The ward grants d8s equal to the sorcery
points spent; the warded creature can expend and roll one of these dice to reduce damage it takes.

**Trance of Order (14th Level)**
Bonus action to enter a trance for 1 minute. Attack rolls against you can't benefit from advantage;
you treat rolls of 9 or lower on your own d20 rolls as a 10. Usable once per long rest or for 5
sorcery points.

**Clockwork Cavalcade (18th Level)**
Action to summon spirits filling a 30-foot cube from you. They restore up to 100 HP (distributed as
you choose), repair damaged objects, and end one spell of 6th level or lower affecting a target
there. Usable once per long rest or for 7 sorcery points.
