# Artificer: Alchemist

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/artificer:alchemist
Fetched: 2026-11-17 (HTTP 200, read in full; no (UA) markers)
Book: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything / Eberron: Rising from the Last War

> **Paraphrased, not verbatim** (fetch tool quote limit). Full coverage of every feature and number.
> One verbatim quote was checked separately and is marked as such.

- **Tool Proficiency (3rd):** alchemist's supplies; if already proficient, one other artisan's tool.
- **Alchemist Spells (3rd):** always prepared, artificer spells, don't count against prepared spells.
  3rd Healing Word, Ray of Sickness · 5th Flaming Sphere, Melf's Acid Arrow · 9th Gaseous Form, Mass
  Healing Word · 13th Blight, Death Ward · 17th Cloudkill, Raise Dead.
- **Experimental Elixir (3rd):** at the end of a long rest, magically make an elixir in an empty flask you
  touch and roll on the table. Drinking it, or giving it to an incapacitated creature, is an action.
  Extra elixirs: expend a spell slot of 1st level or higher each, using your action — verbatim:
  "you choose the elixir's effect from the Experimental Elixir table." Needs alchemist's supplies on your
  person. An elixir lasts until drunk or the end of your next long rest. 2 elixirs per long rest at 6th
  level, 3 at 15th (each rolled separately, each needs its own flask).
  d6: 1 Healing — regain 2d4 + INT mod HP · 2 Swiftness — +10 ft. walking speed for 1 hour ·
  3 Resilience — +1 AC for 10 minutes · 4 Boldness — add a d4 to every attack roll and saving throw for
  1 minute · 5 Flight — 10 ft. flying speed for 10 minutes · 6 Transformation — as alter self, drinker
  chooses, 10 minutes.
- **Alchemical Savant (5th):** casting a spell with alchemist's supplies as the focus, add INT mod
  (minimum +1) to one roll that restores HP or deals acid, fire, necrotic, or poison damage.
- **Restorative Reagents (9th):** an elixir's drinker also gains 2d6 + INT mod (minimum 1) temp HP; cast
  lesser restoration without a slot or preparing it (alchemist's supplies as focus) INT mod times
  (minimum once), regained on a long rest.
- **Chemical Mastery (15th):** resistance to acid and poison damage, immunity to the poisoned condition;
  cast greater restoration and heal without a slot, without preparing them and without material
  components (alchemist's supplies as focus), each once per long rest.
