## Extracted content used

**Disciple of the Elements (3rd level)**
Learn elemental disciplines that harness ki. Start knowing Elemental Attunement plus one other discipline of your choice; learn one additional discipline at 6th, 11th, and 17th level (can swap a known discipline for a different one when you learn a new one). At 5th level and beyond, some disciplines let you spend extra ki points to cast their spell at a higher level, up to a maximum ki spend that rises with monk level: 3 ki (levels 5-8), 4 ki (9-12), 5 ki (13-16), 6 ki (17-20).

## Elemental disciplines list (name - ki cost - effect)

- Elemental Attunement - free - create minor sensory elemental effects or manipulate a small amount of elemental material
- Fangs of the Fire Snake - 1 ki - unarmed strike reach +10 ft., deals fire damage
- Shape the Flowing River - 1 ki - reshape ice or water in a 30-ft. area
- Fist of Four Thunders - 2 ki - cast Thunderwave
- Fist of Unbroken Air - 2 ki - ranged blast, 3d10 bludgeoning, pushes the target
- Rush of the Gale Spirits - 2 ki - cast Gust of Wind
- Sweeping Cinder Strike - 2 ki - cast Burning Hands
- Water Whip - 2 ki - whip of water, 3d10 bludgeoning, pulls or knocks prone
- Clench of the North Wind - 3 ki - cast Hold Person
- Gong of the Summit - 3 ki - cast Shatter
- Flames of the Phoenix - 4 ki - cast Fireball
- Mist Stance - 4 ki - cast Gaseous Form on yourself
- Ride the Wind - 4 ki - cast Fly on yourself
- Eternal Mountain Defense - 5 ki - cast Stoneskin on yourself
- River of Hungry Flame - 5 ki - cast Wall of Fire
- Breath of Winter - 6 ki - cast Cone of Cold
- Wave of Rolling Earth - 6 ki - cast Wall of Stone
