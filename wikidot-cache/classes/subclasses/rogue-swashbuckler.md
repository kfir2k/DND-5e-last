## Fancy Footwork (3rd level)
"During your turn, if you make a melee attack against a creature, that creature can't make
opportunity attacks against you for the rest of your turn."

## Rakish Audacity (3rd level)
"You can give yourself a bonus to your initiative rolls equal to your Charisma modifier."
Additionally, you can use Sneak Attack against a target within 5 ft. of you without advantage, as
long as no other creature is within 5 ft. of you and you don't have disadvantage on the attack roll.

## Panache (9th level)
Make a contested Charisma (Persuasion) check against a target's Wisdom (Insight) check. If it's
hostile and you succeed, "it has disadvantage on attack rolls against targets other than you and
can't make opportunity attacks against targets other than you" for 1 minute. If it's not hostile and
you succeed, it's charmed by you and treats you as a friendly acquaintance for 1 minute.

## Elegant Maneuver (13th level)
"You can use a bonus action on your turn to gain advantage on the next Dexterity (Acrobatics) or
Strength (Athletics) check you make during the same turn."

## Master Duelist (17th level)
"If you miss with an attack roll, you can roll it again with advantage. Once you do so, you can't
use this feature again until you finish a short or long rest."
