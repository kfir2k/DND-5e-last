# Shadow Magic (Sorcerer)

Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Class Features

**Eyes of the Dark (1st Level)**
Grants 120-foot darkvision. At 3rd level, learn the Darkness spell (doesn't count against spells
known). Cast it using 2 sorcery points or a spell slot; if cast with sorcery points, you can see
through the darkness created by the spell.

**Strength of the Grave (1st Level)**
When damage reduces you to 0 hit points, make a Charisma save (DC 5 + damage taken). Success means
you drop to 1 HP instead. Cannot be used if reduced to 0 HP by radiant damage or a critical hit.
Recharges after a long rest.

**Hound of Ill Omen (6th Level)**
Bonus action, spend 3 sorcery points to summon a hound within 120 feet of a target. Uses dire wolf
stats, modified as: Medium size, monstrosity type, temporary HP equal to half your sorcerer level,
can move through creatures/objects (takes 5 force damage if it ends its turn inside an object),
automatically knows the target's location, lasts 5 minutes or until reduced to 0 HP. The hound
moves only toward the target by the most direct route, attacks only the target, and can make
opportunity attacks only against the target. While the hound is within 5 feet of the target, the
target has disadvantage on saving throws against any spell you cast.

**Shadow Walk (14th Level)**
Bonus action while in dim light or darkness: teleport up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space you can
see that is also in dim light or darkness.

**Umbral Form (18th Level)**
Spend 6 sorcery points as a bonus action to transform into a shadowy form for 1 minute. Gain
resistance to all damage except force and radiant; move through creatures and objects as difficult
terrain (5 force damage if you end your turn inside an object).
