## Features by level

**Level 3 — Profane Soul Spellcasting**
Learn 2 cantrips and 2 1st-level spells from the warlock spell list; gain 1 warlock-style spell
slot (more at higher levels, warlock-style short-rest recovery). Spellcasting ability is your
chosen hemocraft ability (Intelligence or Wisdom). Spell save DC = 8 + prof. bonus + hemocraft
modifier; spell attack modifier = prof. bonus + hemocraft modifier.

**Level 3 — Otherworldly Patron**
Choose a patron from the same list warlocks use: Archfey, Celestial, Fathomless, Fiend, Genie,
Great Old One, Hexblade, Undead, or Undying. The choice determines your Rite Focus benefit.

**Level 3 — Rite Focus**
While a Crimson Rite is active, your weapon can serve as a spellcasting focus for your warlock
spells, and grants a benefit tied to your chosen patron (e.g. imposing disadvantage on being seen,
healing, slowing the target, rerolling damage, brief flight, fear, extra curse damage, damage
reduction, or HP recovery — varies by patron).

**Level 7 — Mystic Frenzy**
When you use your action to cast a cantrip, you can make one weapon attack as a bonus action.

**Level 7 — Revealed Arcana**
Once per long rest, cast one patron-specific spell (examples: Blur, Lesser Restoration, Gust of
Wind, Scorching Ray, Phantasmal Force, Detect Thoughts, Branding Smite, Blindness/Deafness,
Silence) without expending a spell slot.

**Level 11 — Brand of the Sapping Scar**
A creature branded by you has disadvantage on saving throws against your warlock spells.

**Level 15 — Unsealed Arcana**
Gain a second patron-specific spell (examples: Slow, Revivify, Lightning Bolt, Fireball,
Protection from Energy, Haste, Blink, Speak with Dead, Bestow Curse), castable once per long rest
without a spell slot.

**Level 18 — Blood Curse of the Souleater**
Gain the Blood Curse of the Soul Eater for Blood Maledict (doesn't count against curses known).
