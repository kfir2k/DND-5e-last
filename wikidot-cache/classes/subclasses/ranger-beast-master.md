## 3rd Level: Ranger's Companion

Choose a beast that is no larger than Medium and that has a challenge rating of 1/4 or lower. The ranger adds their proficiency bonus to the beast's AC, attack rolls, damage rolls, saving throws, and skills. Hit points equal the beast's normal maximum or four times ranger level, whichever is higher. The beast obeys commands, takes turns on the ranger's initiative, and can be directed to move or take Attack, Dash, Disengage, or Help actions. Without commands, it takes Dodge. With Extra Attack, the ranger can make one weapon attack when commanding the beast to attack. If the beast dies, the ranger can spend 8 hours bonding with a replacement.

(Optional alternate rule, Tasha's) Primal Companion: the ranger summons a primal beast with three stat block options (Land, Sea, or Sky). The beast acts during the ranger's turn but only takes Dodge unless the ranger uses a bonus action to command another action or sacrifices an attack. The beast can be resummoned after a long rest. If it died within the hour, expending a 1st-level+ spell slot revives it after 1 minute.

## 7th Level: Exceptional Training

On any of your turns when your beast companion doesn't attack, you can use a bonus action to command the beast to take the Dash, Disengage, or Help action. Beast attacks count as magical for overcoming resistance.

## 11th Level: Bestial Fury

When you command your beast companion to take the Attack action, the beast can make two attacks.

## 15th Level: Share Spells

When you cast a spell targeting yourself, you can also affect your beast companion with the spell if the beast is within 30 feet of you.
