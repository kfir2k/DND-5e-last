## Spellcasting (Level 3)
Arcane Tricksters gain spellcasting using Intelligence as their ability. They learn three cantrips:
Mage Hand and two other cantrips of your choice from the wizard spell list. At 10th level they gain
another wizard cantrip.

Spells known: begin with three 1st-level wizard spells of your choice, two of which must be chosen
from the enchantment and illusion spells. Additional spells learned must typically be enchantment or
illusion, except at levels 8, 14, and 20 when they can be chosen from any school.

Save DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + Intelligence modifier. Attack bonus = proficiency bonus +
Intelligence modifier.

## Mage Hand Legerdemain (Level 3)
The spectral hand becomes invisible and can perform three tasks: stow an object in a container worn
by another creature, retrieve an object from such a container, or use thieves' tools to pick a lock
or disarm a trap at range. These actions require a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check contested by the
target's Wisdom (Perception) check to avoid detection. The bonus action from Cunning Action can
control the hand.

## Magical Ambush (Level 9)
"If you are hidden from a creature when you cast a spell on it, the creature has disadvantage on any
saving throw it makes against the spell this turn."

## Versatile Trickster (Level 13)
As a bonus action, designate a creature within 5 feet of the Mage Hand to gain advantage on attack
rolls against that creature until the end of the turn.

## Spell Thief (Level 17)
After a creature casts a spell targeting the rogue, use your reaction to force a save using the
creature's spellcasting ability (DC = rogue's spell save DC). On a failure, the rogue negates the
spell's effect and steals knowledge of it for 8 hours, usable if it's 1st level or higher and on the
wizard spell list. Recharges after a long rest.
