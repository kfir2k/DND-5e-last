# Wild Magic (Sorcerer)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/sorcerer:wild-magic (colon replaced with a dash in filename)
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Class Features

**Wild Magic Surge (1st Level)**
Immediately after casting a 1st-level or higher sorcerer spell, the DM can have you roll d20 once
per turn. On a 1, roll on the Wild Magic Surge table. Triggered effects function at full power,
without Metamagic limitations or concentration requirements.

**Tides of Chaos (1st Level)**
Gain advantage on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw. Usable once per long rest.
Before regaining this feature, the DM can trigger a Wild Magic Surge after you cast a 1st-level or
higher sorcerer spell, which then restores this ability early.

**Bend Luck (6th Level)**
When a visible creature makes an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, spend 2 sorcery
points and use your reaction to roll 1d4. Apply the result as a bonus or penalty (your choice)
after their roll but before the effects of the roll occur.

**Controlled Chaos (14th Level)**
When rolling on the Wild Magic Surge table, roll twice and choose either result.

**Spell Bombardment (18th Level)**
When rolling damage for a spell, if you roll the highest number possible on any damage die, choose
one of the maximum dice, reroll it, and add the new result to the total damage of the spell.
Usable once per turn.

## Wild Magic Surge Table (d100)

A range of 100 possible effects (healing, temporary stat changes, casting a random spell, gaining a
temporary ability, becoming cursed/transformed, regaining resources, etc.), condensed here rather
than reproduced row-by-row, matching the precedent set by `barbarian-wild-magic.md` for its own
(different, 8-entry d8) Wild Magic Surge table. Triggered by rolling a 1 on the d20 described above.
