## Class Features

**Bonus Proficiencies (1st Level)**
"At 1st level, you gain proficiency with martial weapons and heavy armor."

**Eyes of Night (1st Level)**
"Starting at 1st level, you can see through the deepest gloom. You have darkvision out to a range
of 300 feet. In that radius, you can see in dim light as if it were bright light and in darkness
as if it were dim light."

You may share darkvision with willing creatures within 10 feet, up to your Wisdom modifier
(minimum one). "The shared darkvision lasts for 1 hour. Once you share it, you can't do so again
until you finish a long rest, unless you expend a spell slot of any level to share it again."

**Vigilant Blessing (1st Level)**
"At 1st level, the night has taught you to be vigilant. As an action, you give one creature you
touch (including possibly yourself) advantage on the next initiative roll the creature makes."

**Channel Divinity: Twilight Sanctuary (2nd Level)**
Creates a 30-foot-radius sphere of dim light centered on you lasting 1 minute. Grant creatures
ending turns within it either "temporary hit points equal to 1d6 plus your cleric level" or end
one charmed/frightened effect.

**Steps of Night (6th Level)**
"As a bonus action when you are in dim light or darkness, you can magically give yourself a flying
speed equal to your walking speed for 1 minute." Uses equal to proficiency bonus, regained on long
rest.

**Divine Strike (8th Level)**
"Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can cause the attack
to deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage. When you reach 14th level, the extra damage increases to
2d8."

**Twilight Shroud (17th Level)**
"You and your allies have half cover while in the sphere created by your Twilight Sanctuary."
