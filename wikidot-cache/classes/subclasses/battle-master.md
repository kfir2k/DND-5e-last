# Fighter: Battle Master

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/fighter:battle-master
Fetched: 2026-09-11

## Features

**Combat Superiority (3rd level)**
"You learn three maneuvers of your choice... You learn two additional maneuvers of your choice at 7th, 10th, and 15th level." (3 known at 3rd, 5 at 7th, 7 at 10th, 9 at 15th — verbatim confirmed via WebFetch, not from a formatted table on the page.)
"You have four superiority dice, which are d8s... You gain another superiority die at 7th level and one more at 15th level." (4 at 3rd, 5 at 7th, 6 at 15th.)
You can use only one maneuver per attack. Save DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice).

**Student of War (3rd level)**
Gain proficiency with one type of artisan's tools of your choice.

**Know Your Enemy (7th level)**
After 1 minute observing or interacting with a creature outside combat, learn whether it's your equal, superior, or inferior in two characteristics you choose (Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, AC, current HP, total class levels, fighter levels).

**Improved Combat Superiority (10th level, 18th level)**
Superiority dice become d10s at 10th level, d12s at 18th level.

**Relentless (15th level)**
When you roll initiative and have no superiority dice remaining, you regain one superiority die.
