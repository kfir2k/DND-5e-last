## Whispers of the Dead (3rd level)
"Whenever you finish a short or long rest, you can gain one skill or tool proficiency of your
choice, as a ghostly presence shares its knowledge with you." You lose the previous one when you
choose a new one.

## Wails from the Grave (3rd level)
After dealing Sneak Attack damage, "you can target a second creature that you can see within 30 feet
of the first creature. Roll half the number of Sneak Attack dice for your level (round up), and the
second creature takes necrotic damage equal to the roll's total." Recharges on a short/long rest;
uses equal to proficiency bonus.

## Tokens of the Departed (9th level)
"As a reaction when a creature you can see dies within 30 feet of you, you can open your free hand
and cause a Tiny trinket to appear there, a soul trinket." Maximum trinkets held equal to
proficiency bonus. Soul trinkets: grant advantage on death saves and CON saves to whoever holds one,
can trigger Wails from the Grave for free (once per trinket), or let you question the associated
spirit as an action.

## Ghost Walk (13th level)
"As a bonus action, you assume a spectral form. While in this form, you have a flying speed of 10
feet, you can hover, and attack rolls have disadvantage against you." You can move through
creatures/objects but take 1d10 force damage if you end your turn inside one. Lasts 10 minutes;
recharges on a long rest or by destroying a soul trinket.

## Death's Friend (17th level)
"When you use your Wails from the Grave, you can now deal the necrotic damage to both the first and
the second creature." You also gain a soul trinket automatically at the start of each day if you
have none.
