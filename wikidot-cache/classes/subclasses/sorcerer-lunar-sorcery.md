# Lunar Sorcery (Sorcerer)

Source book (confirmed on fetch): Dragonlance: Shadow of the Dragon Queen. Not UA — official.

## Class Features

**Lunar Embodiment (1st Level)**
Grants bonus spells from three lunar phases (Full Moon, New Moon, Crescent Moon). After each long
rest, choose one phase to manifest. You can cast one 1st-level spell of the associated phase (from
the Lunar Spells table) once without expending a spell slot.

**Moon Fire (1st Level)**
Learn the Sacred Flame cantrip (doesn't count against your cantrips known). You can target one
creature as normal, or target two creatures within range that are within 5 feet of each other.

**Lunar Boons (6th Level)**
Each phase associates with two spell schools: Full Moon (Abjuration/Divination), New Moon
(Enchantment/Necromancy), Crescent Moon (Illusion/Transmutation). When you apply Metamagic to a
spell of an associated school, reduce the sorcery points spent by 1 (minimum 0). Uses limited to
proficiency bonus per long rest.

**Waxing and Waning (6th Level)**
As a bonus action, you can spend 1 sorcery point to change your current Lunar Embodiment phase for
a different one. You can now cast one 1st-level spell from each phase (not just your current one)
once per long rest, matching each phase's listed spell.

**Lunar Empowerment (14th Level)**
Passive benefits per phase: Full Moon grants light emission and advantage on Investigation/
Perception checks within it; New Moon provides Stealth advantage and disadvantage on attack rolls
against you while in darkness; Crescent Moon grants resistance to necrotic and radiant damage.

**Lunar Phenomenon (18th Level)**
Bonus action ability per phase with different effects (healing/blinding, damage/slowing,
teleportation/damage resistance). Once you use one of these bonus-action benefits, you can't use
that benefit again until you finish a long rest, unless you spend 5 sorcery points.
