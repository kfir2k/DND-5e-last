## Class Features

**Bonus Cantrip (Level 2)**
"You learn one additional druid cantrip of your choice. This cantrip doesn't count against the
number of druid cantrips you know."

**Natural Recovery (Level 2)**
Once per day during a short rest, regain spell slots with a combined level equal to or less than
half your druid level (rounded up), none of them 6th level or higher. Example: a 4th-level druid
can recover up to two levels' worth of slots. Recharges after a long rest.

**Circle Spells (Levels 3, 5, 7, 9)**
Choose one terrain type at 2nd level; the listed spells for that terrain are always prepared for
you and don't count against your number of prepared spells.

- **Arctic:** Hold Person, Spike Growth (3rd) | Sleet Storm, Slow (5th) | Freedom of Movement, Ice
  Storm (7th) | Commune with Nature, Cone of Cold (9th)
- **Coast:** Mirror Image, Misty Step (3rd) | Water Breathing, Water Walk (5th) | Control Water,
  Freedom of Movement (7th) | Conjure Elemental, Scrying (9th)
- **Desert:** Blur, Silence (3rd) | Create Food and Water, Protection from Energy (5th) | Blight,
  Hallucinatory Terrain (7th) | Insect Plague, Wall of Stone (9th)
- **Forest:** Barkskin, Spider Climb (3rd) | Call Lightning, Plant Growth (5th) | Divination,
  Freedom of Movement (7th) | Commune with Nature, Tree Stride (9th)
- **Grassland:** Invisibility, Pass Without Trace (3rd) | Daylight, Haste (5th) | Divination,
  Freedom of Movement (7th) | Dream, Insect Plague (9th)
- **Mountain:** Spider Climb, Spike Growth (3rd) | Lightning Bolt, Meld into Stone (5th) | Stone
  Shape, Stoneskin (7th) | Passwall, Wall of Stone (9th)
- **Swamp:** Darkness, Melf's Acid Arrow (3rd) | Water Walk, Stinking Cloud (5th) | Freedom of
  Movement, Locate Creature (7th) | Insect Plague, Scrying (9th)
- **Underdark:** Spider Climb, Web (3rd) | Gaseous Form, Stinking Cloud (5th) | Greater
  Invisibility, Stone Shape (7th) | Cloudkill, Insect Plague (9th)

**Land's Stride (Level 6)**
"Moving through nonmagical difficult terrain costs you no extra movement." You can move through
nonmagical plants without being slowed by them and without taking damage from them if they have
thorns, spines, or a similar hazard. You also have advantage on saving throws against plants that
are magically created or manipulated to impede movement.

**Nature's Ward (Level 10)**
You can't be charmed or frightened by elementals or fey, and you're immune to poison and disease.

**Nature's Sanctuary (Level 14)**
A beast or plant creature that attacks you must first succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (against
your spell save DC) or choose a different target; a creature that succeeds is immune to this
effect for 24 hours. This doesn't work against a creature you or your allies have charmed,
frightened, or magically forced to fight.
