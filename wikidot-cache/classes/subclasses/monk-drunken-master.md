## Extracted content used

**Bonus Proficiencies (3rd level)**
Gain proficiency in Performance and brewer's supplies, if you don't already have them.

**Drunken Technique (3rd level)**
Whenever you use Flurry of Blows, you gain the benefit of the Disengage action, and your walking speed increases by 10 feet until the end of the current turn.

**Tipsy Sway (6th level)**
Two benefits:
- Leap to Your Feet: when prone, stand up by spending only 5 feet of movement instead of half your speed.
- Redirect Attack: when a creature misses you with a melee attack roll, spend 1 ki point as a reaction to cause that attack to hit a different creature of your choice within 5 feet of you (not the attacker).

**Drunkard's Luck (11th level)**
When you make an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw and have disadvantage, spend 2 ki points to cancel the disadvantage for that roll.

**Intoxicated Frenzy (17th level)**
When you use Flurry of Blows, you can make up to three additional attacks with it (up to five Flurry of Blows attacks total), provided each attack targets a different creature that turn.
