## Class Features

**Circle Spells (Levels 2, 3, 5, 7, 9)**
The following spells are always prepared and don't count against your prepared spells: Burning
Hands, Cure Wounds (2nd); Flaming Sphere, Scorching Ray (3rd); Plant Growth, Revivify (5th); Aura of
Life, Fire Shield (7th); Flame Strike, Mass Cure Wounds (9th).

**Summon Wildfire Spirit (Level 2)**
Action: expend a Wild Shape use to summon a wildfire spirit in an unoccupied space within 30 feet
(AC 13, HP = 5 + 5 × druid level, speed 30 ft. plus hover flight). Each creature within 10 feet of
the space it appears in makes a Dexterity save, taking 2d6 fire damage on a fail (half on success).
It acts right after your turn on your initiative, defaulting to Dodge unless you command it (no
action required); lasts 1 hour, until dismissed (bonus action), or until it drops to 0 HP.

**Enhanced Bond (Level 6)**
While your wildfire spirit is summoned, when you cast a spell that deals fire damage or restores
hit points, roll a d8 and add it to one damage or healing roll of that spell. Spells with a range
other than self can be cast as if you were in the spirit's space instead of your own.

**Cauterizing Flames (Level 10)**
When a creature dies within 30 feet of you or your wildfire spirit, spectral flames can flare from
its corpse: choose another creature within 30 feet to take 2d10 + your Wisdom modifier fire damage
or regain that many hit points. Usable a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long
rest.

**Blazing Revival (Level 14)**
If you're reduced to 0 hit points while your wildfire spirit is within 120 feet of you, you can
sacrifice it (it's destroyed) to regain hit points equal to half your maximum hit points and stand
back up. Usable once per long rest.
