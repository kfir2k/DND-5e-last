## Extracted content used

**Radiant Sun Bolt (3rd level)**
Gain a ranged spell attack (range 30 ft.) usable with the Attack action, dealing radiant damage that scales with monk level (uses your Martial Arts die). Spend 1 ki point to make the attack twice as a bonus action.

**Searing Arc Strike (6th level)**
Immediately after taking the Attack action, spend 2 ki points to cast Burning Hands as a bonus action. Spend additional ki points to increase the spell's level by 1 each, up to a maximum ki expenditure of half your monk level.

**Searing Sunburst (11th level)**
As an action, create and hurl a radiant orb at a point within 150 feet; it erupts in a 20-foot-radius sphere dealing 2d6 radiant damage (Constitution save for half). Each additional ki point spent, up to 3, adds 2d6 damage.

**Sun Shield (17th level)**
You shed bright light in a 30-foot radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet. When a creature hits you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to deal it 5 + your Wisdom modifier radiant damage.
