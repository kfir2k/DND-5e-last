# Oath of the Crown (Paladin)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/paladin:crown (colon replaced with a dash in filename per this folder's README)
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Oath Spells
- 3rd: Command, Compelled Duel
- 5th: Warding Bond, Zone of Truth
- 9th: Aura of Vitality, Spirit Guardians
- 13th: Banishment, Guardian of Faith
- 17th: Circle of Power, Geas

## Channel Divinity Options (3rd level)
**Champion Challenge:** Bonus action — each creature of your choice that you can see within 30
feet makes a Wisdom save or can't willingly move more than 30 feet away from you (ends if you die,
are incapacitated, or the creature is forced away).

**Turn the Tide:** Bonus action — each creature of your choice that can hear you within 30 feet
regains 1d6 + your Charisma modifier HP (minimum 1) if it's at half its hit points or fewer.

## Divine Allegiance (7th level)
Reaction: when a creature within 5 feet of you takes damage, magically substitute your own health
for the target's — it takes no damage and you take that damage instead. This damage to you can't
be reduced or prevented in any way.

## Unyielding Saint (15th level)
Advantage on saving throws against being paralyzed or stunned.

## Exalted Champion (20th level)
Action: gain the following for 1 hour — resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and
slashing damage; allies within 30 feet have advantage on death saving throws; you and allies
within 30 feet have advantage on Wisdom saving throws. Ends early if you're incapacitated or die.
Once per long rest.
