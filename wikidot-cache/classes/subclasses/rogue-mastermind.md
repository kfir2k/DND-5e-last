# Rogue: Mastermind

Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Master of Intrigue (3rd level)
"You gain proficiency with the disguise kit, the forgery kit, and one gaming set of your choice. You
also learn two languages of your choice." You can also mimic the speech patterns and accent of a
creature you've heard speak for at least one minute.

## Master of Tactics (3rd level)
"You can use the Help action as a bonus action." When helping an ally's attack, the target can be
within 30 feet instead of 5 feet, provided it can see or hear you.

## Insightful Manipulator (9th level)
After observing or interacting with a creature outside combat for at least one minute, you learn if
it's your equal, superior, or inferior in two characteristics you choose: Intelligence score, Wisdom
score, Charisma score, or class levels.

## Misdirection (13th level)
"When you are targeted by an attack while a creature within 5 feet of you is granting you cover
against that attack, you can use your reaction to have the attack target that creature instead of
you."

## Soul of Deceit (17th level)
Your thoughts resist telepathic intrusion unless you permit it. You can present false thoughts via a
contested Charisma (Deception) vs. Wisdom (Insight) check. Truth-detection magic reads you as
truthful at your choice, and you cannot be magically compelled to speak only the truth.
