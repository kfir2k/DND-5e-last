# Path of the Zealot (Barbarian)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/barbarian:zealot (colon replaced with a dash in filename)
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Class Features

**Divine Fury (3rd Level)**
The first creature you hit on each of your turns with a weapon attack takes extra damage equal to
1d6 + half your barbarian level. Choose necrotic or radiant damage when you gain this feature.

**Warrior of the Gods (3rd Level)**
If a spell (such as Raise Dead) has the sole effect of restoring you to life (not undeath), the
caster doesn't need material components to cast it on you.

**Fanatical Focus (6th Level)**
If you fail a saving throw while raging, you can reroll it and must use the new roll. Once per rage.

**Zealous Presence (10th Level)**
Bonus action: unleash a battle cry infused with divine energy. Up to ten other creatures of your
choice within 60 feet who can hear you gain advantage on attack rolls and saving throws until the
start of your next turn.

**Rage Beyond Death (14th Level)**
Having 0 hit points doesn't knock you unconscious; you still make death saving throws and suffer
normal effects of taking damage at 0 HP, but you don't die from failed death saves until your rage
ends.
