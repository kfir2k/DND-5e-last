# Oathbreaker (Paladin)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/paladin:oathbreaker (colon replaced with a dash in filename per this folder's README)
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Oath Spells
- 3rd: Hellish Rebuke, Inflict Wounds
- 5th: Crown of Madness, Darkness
- 9th: Animate Dead, Bestow Curse
- 13th: Blight, Confusion
- 17th: Contagion, Dominate Person

## Channel Divinity Options (3rd level)
**Control Undead:** Action — target one undead creature you can see within 30 feet; it makes a
Wisdom save (immune if its CR is equal to or greater than your paladin level) or obeys your
commands for the next 24 hours or until you use this again.

**Dreadful Aspect:** Action — each creature of your choice within 30 feet that can see you makes a
Wisdom save or is frightened of you for 1 minute; a frightened creature can repeat the save
whenever it moves more than 30 feet from you, ending the effect on a success.

## Aura of Hate (7th level)
You, and fiends and undead within 10 feet of you (30 feet at 18th level), gain a bonus to melee
weapon damage rolls equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum +1). A creature can benefit from only
one Oathbreaker's Aura of Hate at a time.

## Supernatural Resistance (15th level)
Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from nonmagical weapons.

## Dread Lord (20th level)
Action: activate an aura for 1 minute — bright light in a 30-foot radius around you is reduced to
dim light; frightened enemies that start their turn in the aura take 4d10 psychic damage; as a
bonus action you can make a melee spell attack against a creature in the aura, dealing 3d10 +
Charisma modifier necrotic damage. Recharges after a long rest.
