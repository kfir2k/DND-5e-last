# Rogue: Soulknife

## Psionic Power (3rd level)
You harbor a wellspring of psionic energy, represented by psionic energy dice equal to twice your
proficiency bonus. Die size: d6 (3rd level), d8 (5th), d10 (11th), d12 (17th). Regain all expended
dice on a long rest; you can also spend 1 minute during a short/long rest to regain a single die.

Two abilities draw on these dice:
- Psi-Bolstered Knack: if you fail an ability check using a skill/tool you're proficient with, spend
  a die, roll it, and add the result to the check — the die is expended only if this turns it into a
  success.
- Psychic Whispers: spend a die to establish telepathic contact with up to your proficiency bonus in
  creatures you can see within 60 ft., lasting a number of hours equal to the roll (the first use
  each day costs no die).

## Psychic Blades (3rd level)
As a bonus action, manifest a psychic blade in each hand (simple melee weapons with the finesse and
thrown properties, range 60/— ft.). Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma when you take this
subclass — that ability is used for attack and damage rolls with the blades. A hit deals 1d6 psychic
damage. As part of manifesting them (or as a separate bonus action later in the turn), you can also
attack with the second blade, dealing 1d4 psychic damage without adding your ability modifier
(unless it's negative).

## Soul Blades (9th level)
Two more uses for your psionic energy dice:
- Homing Strikes: on a missed attack roll, spend a die, roll it, and add it to the attack roll,
  potentially turning the miss into a hit.
- Psychic Teleportation: spend a die to teleport up to 10 times the number rolled, in feet, to an
  unoccupied space you can see.

## Psychic Veil (13th level)
As an action, turn invisible (along with equipment you're wearing/carrying) for 1 hour, until you
attack or cast a spell, or until you dismiss the effect (no action required). Recharges on a long
rest, or spend one psionic energy die to use it again before then.

## Rend Mind (17th level)
When you deal Sneak Attack damage to a creature, it must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 +
proficiency bonus + Dexterity modifier) or be stunned for up to 1 minute, repeating the save at the
end of each of its turns. Recharges on a long rest, or spend three psionic energy dice to use it
again before then.
