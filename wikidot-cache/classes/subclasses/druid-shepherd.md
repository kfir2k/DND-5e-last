## Class Features

**Speech of the Woods (Level 2)**
Learn Sylvan. You can communicate with beasts and understand their noises, though they lack the
sophistication to convey complex ideas.

**Spirit Totem (Level 2)**
As a bonus action, summon a Tiny spectral animal spirit in an unoccupied space within 60 feet; it
lasts 1 minute, radiates its effect out to 30 feet, and you can move it up to 60 feet as a bonus
action. Usable once per short/long rest. Choose a totem each time:

- **Bear Spirit:** you and allies in range gain temporary hit points equal to 5 + your druid level
  when the totem appears, and have advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws while
  in range.
- **Hawk Spirit:** allies in range have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks; as a reaction, you
  can grant advantage on one ally's attack roll against a creature also within the totem's range.
- **Unicorn Spirit:** you and allies in range have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to
  detect hidden creatures, and whenever you cast a spell that restores hit points to a creature in
  range, that creature regains additional hit points equal to your druid level.

**Mighty Summoner (Level 6)**
Beasts and fey you summon (via spells like Conjure Animals) gain 2 extra hit points per Hit Die and
their natural weapon attacks count as magical for overcoming resistance/immunity.

**Guardian Spirit (Level 10)**
Beasts and fey you've summoned regain hit points equal to half your druid level whenever they end
their turn in your Spirit Totem's radius.

**Faithful Summons (Level 14)**
If you're reduced to 0 hit points or are incapacitated, you can immediately (no action required)
summon four beast spirits (as if you'd cast Conjure Animals as a 9th-level spell) with CR 2 or
lower in unoccupied spaces within 20 feet of you, to protect you; they act immediately after you
and disappear after 1 hour without needing concentration. Usable once per long rest.
