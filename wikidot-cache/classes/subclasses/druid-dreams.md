## Class Features

**Balm of the Summer Court (Level 2)**
You have a pool of fey energy, represented by a number of d6s equal to your druid level. As a
bonus action, spend dice (up to half your druid level's worth at once) on creatures within 120
feet: each restores hit points equal to the dice spent, and each creature also gains 1 temporary
hit point per die spent. The pool refills on a long rest.

**Hearth of Moonlight and Shadow (Level 6)**
While your group rests, create an invisible 30-foot-radius protective sphere centered on the
resting place. Creatures resting within it gain a +5 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) and Wisdom
(Perception) checks, and any light (even open flame) inside is imperceptible from outside the
sphere. Ends when the rest ends or you leave.

**Hidden Paths (Level 10)**
As a bonus action, teleport yourself up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space you can see, or use your
action to teleport one willing creature you touch up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can
see. Uses equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1) per long rest.

**Walker in Dreams (Level 14)**
After finishing a short rest, cast Dream, Scrying, or Teleportation Circle without a spell slot or
material components; the Teleportation Circle version links to a permanent circle at the site of
your last long rest on your current plane. Usable once, recharging after a long rest.
