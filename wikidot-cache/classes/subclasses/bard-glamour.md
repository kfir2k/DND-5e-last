## Class Features

**Level 3 - Mantle of Inspiration**
Bonus action: expend one use of your Bardic Inspiration to grant yourself a wondrous appearance.
Choose a number of creatures you can see within 60 ft. up to your CHA modifier (minimum one) —
each gains 5 temporary hit points (8 at 5th level, 11 at 10th, 14 at 15th) and can immediately use
its reaction to move up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Level 3 - Enthralling Performance**
Perform for at least 1 minute; a number of humanoids who watched the whole performance (up to
your CHA modifier) must succeed on a WIS save (your spell save DC) or be charmed by you for 1
hour, idolizing you and hesitant to harm you or your allies. Ends early if a charmed creature
takes damage, you attack it, or it witnesses you attack an ally. Once per short or long rest.

**Level 6 - Mantle of Majesty**
Bonus action: cast Command without a spell slot, and gain an unearthly appearance for 1 minute;
each turn during it, cast Command again as a bonus action with no slot needed. Creatures already
charmed by you automatically fail their saves against these castings. Once per long rest.

**Level 14 - Unbreakable Majesty**
Bonus action: assume a magically majestic presence for 1 minute or until incapacitated. Each time
a creature attacks you while it's active, before the attack roll it must succeed on a CHA save
(your spell save DC) or be unable to make that attack; on a success it instead has disadvantage
on its next saving throw against your spells before your next turn. Once per short or long rest.
