# Graviturgy Magic (Wizard)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/wizard:graviturgy (colon replaced with a dash in filename)
Fetched: 2026-09-05
Note: Explorer's Guide to Wildemount subclass.

## Class Features

**Adjust Density (2nd Level)**
As an action, choose one Large or smaller creature or object within 30 feet; concentrate up to 1
minute to halve or double its weight. Halved: +10 ft. speed, double jump distance, disadvantage on
Strength checks/saves. Doubled: -10 ft. speed, advantage on Strength checks/saves. At 10th level,
this can affect Huge or smaller targets.

**Gravity Well (6th Level)**
When you cast a spell that requires an attack roll or a saving throw against a creature, you can
pull that target 5 feet toward you into an unoccupied space, if the attack hits, the target fails
its save, or the target is willing.

**Violent Attraction (10th Level)**
Reaction, when a creature within 60 feet hits with a weapon attack, or takes falling damage: increase
that damage by 1d10 of the weapon's damage type (weapon hit) or 2d10 (falling damage). Uses per long
rest equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1).

**Event Horizon (14th Level)**
As an action, emit a 30-ft. gravitational field around yourself for up to 1 minute (concentration).
Each hostile creature that starts its turn in the field must succeed on a Strength save (vs. your
spell save DC) or take 2d10 force damage and have its speed reduced to 0 until the start of its
next turn; on a success, it takes half damage and every foot it moves costs an extra foot. Recharges
on a long rest, or by expending a spell slot of 3rd level or higher.
