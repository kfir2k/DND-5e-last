# School of Transmutation (Wizard)

## Class Features

**Transmutation Savant (2nd Level)**
The gold and time you must spend to copy a transmutation spell into your spellbook is halved.

**Minor Alchemy (2nd Level)**
Over the course of 10 minutes per cubic foot, you can temporarily transform one kind of nonmagical
material (wood, stone, iron, copper, or silver) into another. The transformation lasts 1 hour, until
you lose concentration (as if concentrating on a spell), or until the created material takes any
damage, after which it reverts.

**Transmuter's Stone (6th Level)**
Spend 8 hours to create a transmuter's stone; while a creature carries it, it grants one benefit of
your choice: darkvision out to 60 feet, +10 ft. speed while not encumbered, proficiency in
Constitution saves, or resistance to acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage (your choice).
Each time you cast a transmutation spell of 1st level or higher, you may change the stone's granted
benefit if the stone is on your person.

**Shapechanger (10th Level)**
You add Polymorph to your spellbook if it isn't there already. Once per short or long rest, you can
cast it without expending a spell slot, but you may target only yourself and transform only into a
beast with a challenge rating of 1 or lower.

**Master Transmuter (14th Level)**
As an action, destroy a transmuter's stone in your possession for one effect: transform up to a
5-foot cube of nonmagical material into another nonmagical material of your choice (10 minutes
handling); cast Raise Dead without a spell slot or material components; remove all curses,
diseases, and poisons and restore up to 100 HP to one willing creature; or reduce a willing
creature's age by 3d10 years (minimum 13).
