# Order of Scribes (Wizard)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/wizard:order-of-scribes (colon replaced with a dash in filename)
Fetched: 2026-09-05
Note: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything subclass.

## Class Features

**Wizardly Quill (2nd Level)**
As a bonus action, conjure a magic quill (lasts until you conjure another or die). While holding it:
write with ink of any color you like without needing ink; copy a spell into a spellbook in 2 minutes
per level of the spell instead of the normal time (still costs the normal gold); and erase writing
it made within 5 feet as a bonus action.

**Awakened Spellbook (2nd Level)**
Your spellbook becomes a magical spellcasting focus for your wizard spells. While holding it: when
you cast a wizard spell that deals damage, you can change its damage type to one used by another
spell already in your spellbook; you can cast a wizard ritual spell from it using its normal casting
time (instead of adding 10 minutes) once per long rest; and if the book is destroyed or lost, you can
make a new one with your Wizardly Quill over a short rest, copying only spells you can currently
prepare/know.

**Manifest Mind (6th Level)**
As a bonus action, manifest your spellbook's awareness as a Tiny, invisible-to-nobody spectral mind
within 60 feet, sheding dim light in a 10-ft. radius and with darkvision 60 ft.; it shares what it
senses with you telepathically, and you can cast spells with a range of touch or greater as if you
were in its space, using your own senses or its. Move it up to 30 feet as a bonus action. It lasts
until dismissed (bonus action), the book is destroyed, you die, or it ends up more than 300 feet from
the book. Usable a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest, recharging one use
whenever you cast a spell using a spell slot.

**Master Scrivener (10th Level)**
After a long rest, use your Wizardly Quill to create a spell scroll of a 1st- or 2nd-level spell from
your spellbook that has a casting time of an action; casting it from the scroll uses your normal
spellcasting ability and treats the spell as one level higher than normal. The scroll can't be read by
anyone else and disappears after your next long rest if unused. Copying a spell scroll costs half the
normal gold and time when you use your quill.

**One with the Word (14th Level)**
While your spellbook is on your person, you have advantage on Intelligence (Arcana) checks. When your
Manifest Mind would take damage, you can use your reaction to dismiss it and negate the damage; you
then roll 3d6, and spells of that combined level or lower vanish from your mind/prepared spells for
1d6 long rests (spells already cast are unaffected). Recharges after a long rest.
