# School of Abjuration (Wizard)

## Class Features

**Abjuration Savant (2nd Level)**
The gold and time you must spend to copy an abjuration spell into your spellbook is halved.

**Arcane Ward (2nd Level)**
When you cast an abjuration spell of 1st level or higher, you create a magical ward with hit points
equal to twice your wizard level plus your Intelligence modifier. The ward absorbs damage that
would otherwise be dealt to you until it is depleted, at which point you take any remaining damage.
While the ward has 0 hit points, it can't absorb damage, but its hit points are added to when you
cast an abjuration spell again — increasing by twice the spell's level. You can have only one
ward at a time; it lasts until you finish a long rest.

**Projected Ward (6th Level)**
When a creature that you can see within 30 feet of you takes damage, you can use your reaction to
cause your Arcane Ward to absorb that damage instead.

**Improved Abjuration (10th Level)**
Whenever you cast an abjuration spell that requires you to make an ability check as part of casting
it (such as Counterspell or Dispel Magic), you add your proficiency bonus to that ability check.

**Spell Resistance (14th Level)**
You have advantage on saving throws against spells, and you have resistance against damage from
spells.
