## 3rd Level - Draconic Gift

Learn the Thaumaturgy cantrip (counts as a ranger spell), and gain literacy in Draconic or one other language of your choice.

## 3rd Level - Drake Companion

Action: summon a bound drake within 30 feet. It shares your initiative but acts after you, defaulting to Dodge unless commanded otherwise. Remains until reduced to 0 HP, resummoned, or you die. Resummoning requires a long rest or expending a 1st-level+ spell slot.

Stats: AC 14 + your proficiency bonus, HP 5 + (5 x ranger level), speed 40 ft., STR 16 DEX 12 CON 15 INT 8 WIS 14 CHA 8, darkvision 60 ft., speaks Draconic.

On summoning, choose a damage type: acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison (its Draconic Essence). Bite: +3 plus your proficiency bonus to hit, 1d6 + proficiency bonus piercing damage. Infused Strikes (reaction): when an ally within 30 feet hits a target, add 1d6 damage of the chosen type.

## 7th Level - Bond of Fang and Scale

The drake gains wings and a flying speed equal to its walking speed, grows to Medium size, and can serve as a mount. Its Bite deals an extra 1d6 damage of the chosen type. You gain resistance to the chosen damage type.

## 11th Level - Drake's Breath

Action: the drake exhales a 30-foot cone. Creatures make a Dexterity save against your spell save DC, taking 8d6 damage (10d6 at 15th level) of the chosen type on a failed save, half as much on a success. Usable once per long rest, or by expending a 3rd-level+ spell slot.

## 15th Level - Perfected Bond

The drake's Bite extra damage increases to 2d6 total. It grows to Large size and can use its flying speed while ridden. You gain a reaction, usable a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus (regained on a long rest), to grant resistance to damage taken by either you or the drake within 30 feet of each other.
