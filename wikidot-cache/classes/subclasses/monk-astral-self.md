## Extracted content used

**Arms of the Astral Self (3rd level)**
Bonus action: spend 1 ki point to summon the arms of your astral self for 10 minutes. Creatures within 10 feet must succeed on a Dexterity save or take force damage equal to two rolls of your Martial Arts die. Benefits: use Wisdom instead of Strength for checks/saves, unarmed strikes gain 5 extra feet of reach, and you can use Wisdom for attack/damage rolls with the arms (force damage type).

**Visage of the Astral Self (6th level)**
Spend 1 ki point to summon this visage for 10 minutes. Grants: normal sight in darkness up to 120 ft., advantage on Insight and Intimidation checks, and the ability to direct speech to one creature within 60 feet or amplify voice to 600 feet.

**Body of the Astral Self (11th level)**
Appears automatically when both arms and visage are active. Two benefits: reduce acid/cold/fire/force/lightning/thunder damage taken by 1d10 + Wisdom modifier (minimum reduction of 1); and once per turn, deal extra damage equal to your Martial Arts die when you hit with an astral-arms unarmed strike.

**Awakened Astral Self (17th level)**
Spend 5 ki points to summon the arms, visage, and body together for 10 minutes. Grants +2 AC, and lets you make three attacks (instead of two) with Extra Attack when every attack uses the astral arms.
