## Extracted content used

**Shadow Arts (3rd level)**
Spend 2 ki points to cast Darkness, Darkvision, Pass without Trace, or Silence, without providing material components. Also learn the Minor Illusion cantrip.

**Shadow Step (6th level)**
When you are in dim light or darkness, as a bonus action you can teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space you can see that is also in dim light or darkness. Gain advantage on the first melee attack you make before the end of the turn.

**Cloak of Shadows (11th level)**
When you are in an area of dim light or darkness, use your action to become invisible. Invisibility ends if you attack, cast a spell, or enter an area of bright light.

**Opportunist (17th level)**
Whenever a creature within 5 feet of you is hit by an attack made by a creature other than you, use your reaction to make a melee attack against that creature.
