# The Undying (Warlock subclass)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/warlock:undying
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Expanded Spell List

1st: False Life, Ray of Sickness. 2nd: Blindness/Deafness, Silence. 3rd: Feign Death, Speak with
Dead. 4th: Aura of Life, Death Ward. 5th: Contagion, Legend Lore.

## Class Features

**Among the Dead (Level 1)**
Learn the Spare the Dying cantrip; it doesn't count against your cantrips known. Advantage on
saving throws against disease. When an undead creature targets you directly with an attack or
harmful spell, it must make a Wisdom save against your spell save DC or choose a new target (or
lose the attack/spell if there's no other target); on a success it's immune to this effect from you
for 24 hours.

**Defy Death (Level 6)**
When you succeed on a death saving throw, or stabilize a creature with Spare the Dying, you regain
HP equal to 1d8 + your Constitution modifier (minimum 1). Once per long rest.

**Undying Nature (Level 10)**
Hold your breath indefinitely; don't need to eat, drink, or sleep (though you still benefit from
rest, and finish long rests normally). You age at one-tenth the normal rate and are immune to
magical aging effects.

**Indestructible Life (Level 14)**
Bonus action: regain HP equal to 1d8 + your warlock level; you can also use this to reattach a
severed body part (if it's still present) instead of healing. Once per short or long rest.
