## Class Features

**Level 3 - Mote of Potential**
Whenever you give a creature a Bardic Inspiration die, you also create a Tiny mote of potential
tied to it, with an extra effect depending on how the die gets used:
- Ability check: the creature can roll the Bardic Inspiration die again and choose which roll to use.
- Attack roll: the target of the attack and creatures within 5 ft. of it make a CON save or take
  thunder damage equal to the die roll.
- Saving throw: the creature gains temporary hit points equal to the die roll plus your CHA
  modifier (minimum 1).

**Level 3 - Performance of Creation**
Action. Create one nonmagical item of your choice in an unoccupied space within 10 ft. of you.
Maximum value: 20 gp x your bard level. Size: Medium or smaller (Large at 6th level, Huge at
14th level). Only one such item can exist at a time; it disappears after a number of hours equal
to your proficiency bonus. Recharges on a long rest, or you can expend a spell slot of 2nd level
or higher to use it again.

**Level 6 - Animating Performance**
Action. Animate one Large or smaller nonmagical item within 30 ft. of you, using the Dancing Item
stat block (AC 16, HP 10 + 5 x your bard level) for 1 hour; only one animated item at a time.
Recharges on a long rest, or you can expend a spell slot of 3rd level or higher to use it again.

**Level 14 - Creative Crescendo**
Performance of Creation can create a number of items simultaneously equal to your CHA modifier
(minimum two), and is no longer limited by gp value.
