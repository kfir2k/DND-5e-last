# The Hexblade (Warlock subclass)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/warlock:hexblade
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Expanded Spell List

1st: Shield, Wrathful Smite. 2nd: Blur, Branding Smite. 3rd: Blink, Elemental Weapon. 4th:
Phantasmal Killer, Staggering Smite. 5th: Banishing Smite, Cone of Cold.

## Class Features

**Hex Warrior (Level 1)**
Proficiency with medium armor, shields, and martial weapons. When you take this feature, and again
whenever you finish a long rest, you can touch one weapon you're proficient with that lacks the
two-handed property; while holding it you can use your Charisma modifier instead of Strength or
Dexterity for its attack and damage rolls. This benefit extends to any weapon summoned by Pact of
the Blade.

**Hexblade's Curse (Level 1)**
Bonus action: curse a creature you can see within 30 ft. for 1 minute (ends early if the target or
you drop to 0 HP or the target dies). While cursed: you add your proficiency bonus to damage rolls
against it, your critical hit range against it is 19-20, and if it dies while cursed you regain HP
equal to your warlock level + Charisma modifier. Once per short or long rest.

**Accursed Specter (Level 6)**
When you slay a humanoid, you can bind its spirit as a specter under your command (a spectral
version of a specter statblock) with temp HP equal to half your warlock level; it gains a bonus
to its attack rolls equal to your Charisma modifier. It remains until the end of your next long
rest. Once per long rest.

**Armor of Hexes (Level 10)**
Reaction when the creature cursed by your Hexblade's Curse hits you with an attack roll: roll a d6
— on a 4 or higher, the attack instead misses you.

**Master of Hexes (Level 14)**
When the creature cursed by your Hexblade's Curse dies, you can transfer the curse to a different
creature you can see within 30 ft. (no action required); you don't regain HP from the previous
target's death when transferring the curse this way. You can't be incapacitated to use this.
