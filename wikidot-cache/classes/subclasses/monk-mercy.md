## Extracted content used

**Implements of Mercy (3rd level)**
Proficiency in Insight and Medicine, and with the herbalism kit. You receive a special mask (appearance rolled on the Merciful Mask table: raven, blank white, crying, laughing, skull, or butterfly).

**Hands of Healing (3rd level)**
As an action, spend 1 ki point to touch a creature and restore hit points equal to a roll of your Martial Arts die + your Wisdom modifier. Can replace one Flurry of Blows unarmed strike without spending ki.

**Hands of Harm (3rd level)**
When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, spend 1 ki point to deal extra necrotic damage equal to a roll of your Martial Arts die + your Wisdom modifier. Once per turn.

**Physician's Touch (6th level)**
Hands of Healing can also end one disease or one of these conditions: blinded, deafened, paralyzed, poisoned, or stunned. Hands of Harm can instead inflict the poisoned condition until the end of your next turn.

**Flurry of Healing and Harm (11th level)**
Flurry of Blows strikes can be replaced with Hands of Healing without spending ki, and unarmed strikes made during Flurry of Blows can trigger Hands of Harm without spending ki (still limited to once per turn).

**Hand of Ultimate Mercy (17th level)**
Touch a corpse that died within the last 24 hours and spend 5 ki points to restore it to life with 4d10 + your Wisdom modifier hit points, removing the blinded, deafened, paralyzed, poisoned, and stunned conditions. Usable once per long rest.
