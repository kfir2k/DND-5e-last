## 3rd Level - Horizon Walker Magic

Bonus spells at specific levels: Protection from Evil and Good (3rd), Misty Step (5th), Haste (9th), Banishment (13th), and Teleportation Circle (17th).

## 3rd Level - Detect Portal

As an action, magically sense the distance and direction to the closest planar portal within 1 mile of you. Usable once per short or long rest.

## 3rd Level - Planar Warrior

Bonus action: choose one creature you can see within 30 feet of you. The next time you hit that creature this turn with a weapon attack, all damage dealt by the attack becomes force damage, and the creature takes an extra 1d8 force damage from the attack. Damage increases to 2d8 at 11th level.

## 7th Level - Ethereal Step

Bonus action: cast Etherealness without expending a spell slot, but the spell ends at the end of the current turn. Usable once per short or long rest.

## 11th Level - Distant Strike

Teleport up to 10 feet before each attack to an unoccupied space you can see. If you attack at least two different creatures with the action, you can make one additional attack with it against a third creature.

## 15th Level - Spectral Defense

When you take damage from an attack, you can use your reaction to give yourself resistance to all of that attack's damage on this turn.
