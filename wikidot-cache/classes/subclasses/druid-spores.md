## Class Features

**Circle Spells (Levels 2, 3, 5, 7, 9)**
Learn the Chill Touch cantrip at 2nd level (doesn't count against cantrips known). The following
spells are always prepared and don't count against your prepared spells: Blindness/Deafness, Gentle
Repose (3rd); Animate Dead, Gaseous Form (5th); Blight, Confusion (7th); Cloudkill, Contagion (9th).

**Halo of Spores (Level 2)**
Reaction: when a creature you can see enters within 10 feet of you, or starts its turn there, you
can force a Constitution save — on a fail it takes 1d4 necrotic damage (usable once per turn). This
damage increases to 1d6 at 6th level, 1d8 at 10th, and 1d10 at 14th.

**Symbiotic Entity (Level 2)**
Bonus action: expend a Wild Shape use to gain 4 temporary hit points per druid level for 10
minutes; while active, Halo of Spores damage dice double, and once per turn on a melee hit you deal
an extra 1d6 necrotic damage.

**Fungal Infestation (Level 6)**
When a Small or Medium beast or humanoid dies within 10 feet of you, you can animate its corpse as
a zombie under your control (1 hit point, obeys your commands, lasts 1 hour). Uses equal to your
Wisdom modifier (minimum 1) per long rest.

**Spreading Spores (Level 10)**
Bonus action: launch spores into a 10-foot cube within 30 feet, filling it for 1 minute; a creature
that enters or starts its turn there takes Halo of Spores' damage (once per turn per creature).
Uses equal to your Wild Shape uses.

**Fungal Body (Level 14)**
Immune to being blinded, deafened, frightened, and poisoned; critical hits against you count as
normal hits instead.
