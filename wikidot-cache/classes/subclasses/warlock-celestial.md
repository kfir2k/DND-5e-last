# The Celestial (Warlock subclass)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/warlock:celestial
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Expanded Spell List

Levels 1-5 include Cure Wounds, Guiding Bolt, Flaming Sphere, Lesser Restoration, Daylight,
Revivify, Guardian of Faith, Wall of Fire, Flame Strike, Greater Restoration.

## Class Features

**Bonus Cantrips (Level 1)**
Learn the Light and Sacred Flame cantrips; they don't count against your cantrips known.

**Healing Light (Level 1)**
Pool of d6s equal to 1 + your warlock level. Bonus action: heal one creature within 60 ft.,
spending up to your Charisma modifier's worth of dice, rolling and restoring that much HP. Pool
refills on a long rest.

**Radiant Soul (Level 6)**
Resistance to radiant damage. When you cast a spell that deals radiant or fire damage, add your
Charisma modifier to the damage it deals on one target.

**Celestial Resistance (Level 10)**
After a short or long rest, you and up to five creatures you choose within 30 ft. gain temporary
HP equal to your warlock level + Charisma modifier (yourself) / half your warlock level + Charisma
modifier (others).

**Searing Vengeance (Level 14)**
When making a death saving throw, you can instead spend it to regain HP equal to half your max HP
and stand up. Each creature within 30 ft. of your choice takes 2d8 + Charisma modifier radiant
damage and is blinded until the end of your next turn. Once per long rest.
