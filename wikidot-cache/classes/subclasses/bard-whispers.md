## Class Features

**Level 3 - Psychic Blades**
Once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can expend one use of Bardic
Inspiration to deal an extra 2d6 psychic damage (3d6 at 5th level, 5d6 at 10th, 8d6 at 15th).

**Level 3 - Words of Terror**
After conversing with a humanoid for at least 1 minute, you can try to seed terror in it: it makes
a WIS save (your spell save DC) or is frightened of you for 1 hour. Ends early if it's attacked or
takes damage. Once per short or long rest.

**Level 6 - Mantle of Whispers**
Reaction, when a humanoid dies within 30 ft. of you: magically capture its shadow, which you can
later wear as a disguise for 1 hour, granting access to its basic knowledge. An observer can see
through the disguise with an Insight check opposed by your Deception check (+5 bonus).

**Level 14 - Shadow Lore**
Action: whisper a phrase only one creature within 30 ft. can hear; it makes a WIS save (your spell
save DC) or is charmed by you for 8 hours, believing you know its deepest secrets and obeying your
commands out of fear of exposure (won't take obviously suicidal action). Once per long rest.
