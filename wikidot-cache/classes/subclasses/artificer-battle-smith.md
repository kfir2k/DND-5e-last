# Artificer: Battle Smith

Book: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything / Eberron: Rising from the Last War

> **Paraphrased, not verbatim** (fetch tool quote limit). Full coverage of every feature, number and stat.

- **Tool Proficiency (3rd):** smith's tools; if already proficient, one other artisan's tool.
- **Battle Smith Spells (3rd):** always prepared, don't count against prepared spells. 3rd Heroism, Shield ·
  5th Branding Smite, Warding Bond · 9th Aura of Vitality, Conjure Barrage · 13th Aura of Purity, Fire Shield ·
  17th Banishing Smite, Mass Cure Wounds.
- **Battle Ready (3rd):** proficiency with martial weapons; with a magic weapon, use INT instead of STR or DEX
  for attack and damage rolls.
- **Steel Defender (3rd):** friendly construct companion; shares your initiative but acts right after you;
  moves and uses reactions on its own; its only action is Dodge unless you use a bonus action to command
  another action; if you're incapacitated it acts freely. Mending restores 2d6 HP. If it died within the last
  hour, use smith's tools as an action within 5 ft. and expend a 1st-level+ spell slot: it returns after 1
  minute with full HP. After a long rest, with smith's tools, make a new one (the old one ends). It dies if you die.
  - Stat block (Medium construct): AC 15 (natural armor); HP 2 + INT mod + 5 × artificer level (d8 Hit Dice
    equal to artificer level); speed 40 ft.; STR 14, DEX 12, CON 14, INT 4, WIS 10, CHA 6; saves DEX +1 + PB,
    CON +2 + PB; skills Athletics +2 + PB, Perception +0 + 2×PB; immune to poison damage; immune to charmed,
    exhaustion, poisoned; darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10 + 2×PB; understands your languages;
    challenge —; PB equals yours.
  - Vigilant: can't be surprised.
  - Force-Empowered Rend: melee weapon attack, your spell attack modifier to hit, reach 5 ft., one target it can
    see; 1d8 + PB force.
  - Repair (3/day): restores 2d8 + PB HP to itself or one construct or object within 5 ft.
  - Deflect Attack (reaction): imposes disadvantage on one attack roll of a creature it can see within 5 ft.,
    provided the attack targets a creature other than the defender.
- **Extra Attack (5th):** attack twice when you take the Attack action.
- **Arcane Jolt (9th):** when your magic weapon attack or your steel defender hits: the target takes an extra
  2d6 force, or one creature or object you can see within 30 ft. of the target regains 2d6 HP. Once per turn;
  uses = INT mod (minimum 1) per long rest.
- **Improved Defender (15th):** Arcane Jolt becomes 4d6; the steel defender gets +2 AC; when it uses Deflect
  Attack, the attacker takes 1d4 + INT mod force damage.
