## Extracted content used

**Draconic Disciple (3rd level)**
- "Draconic Presence": Reroll failed Charisma (Intimidation) or (Persuasion) checks via reaction; usable once per long rest
- "Draconic Strike": Change unarmed strike damage to acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison
- "Tongue of Dragons": Learn Draconic or another language

**Breath of the Dragon (3rd level)**
Replace one Attack action attack with a cone (20 ft.) or line (30 ft. x 5 ft.) breath weapon. Creatures make Dexterity saves against your ki save DC, taking two rolls of your Martial Arts die on failure, half on success. Scales to three dice at 11th level. Usable proficiency-bonus times per long rest, or spend 2 ki points for additional uses.

**Wings Unfurled (6th level)**
During Step of the Wind, manifest spectral draconic wings granting flying speed equal to walking speed until the end of the turn. Usable proficiency-bonus times per long rest.

**Aspect of the Wyrm (11th level)**
Bonus action to create a 10-foot aura lasting 1 minute. Choose either:
- "Frightful Presence": Creatures within make Wisdom saves or become frightened
- "Resistance": You and allies gain resistance to your chosen damage type (acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison)
Reusable after a long rest, or spend 3 ki points for an additional use.

**Ascendant Aspect (17th level)**
- Augment Breath: Spend 1 ki point for a 60-foot cone or 90-foot line; damage becomes four rolls of your Martial Arts die
- Blindsight out to 10 feet
- Explosive Fury: Creatures in the Aspect of the Wyrm aura make Dexterity saves or take 3d10 damage (type of your choice)
