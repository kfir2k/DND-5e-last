# The Fathomless (Warlock subclass)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/warlock:fathomless
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Expanded Spell List

Levels 1-5 include water-themed spells such as Create or Destroy Water, Control Water, and Cone of
Cold.

## Class Features

**Tentacle of the Deep (Level 1)**
Bonus action: create a spectral tentacle in an unoccupied space within 60 ft. As part of the same
bonus action, make a melee spell attack against a creature within 10 ft. of the tentacle: 1d8 cold
damage (2d8 at 10th level) and the target's speed is reduced by 10 ft. until the start of your next
turn. You can move the tentacle up to 30 ft. as a bonus action and attack again. Uses equal to your
proficiency bonus, regained on a long rest.

**Gift of the Sea (Level 1)**
Swimming speed of 40 ft.; you can breathe underwater.

**Oceanic Soul (Level 6)**
Resistance to cold damage; you and creatures you choose can communicate verbally with each other
while underwater.

**Guardian Coil (Level 6)**
Reaction: while your tentacle is within 10 ft. of you or an ally, reduce damage to that target by
1d8 (2d8 at 10th level).

**Grasping Tentacles (Level 10)**
Learn Evard's Black Tentacles; it doesn't count against your spells known and you can cast it once
per long rest without a spell slot. When you cast it this way you also gain temporary HP equal to
your warlock level, and it can't be countered/dispelled by breaking your concentration voluntarily
in the normal way (per the class text).

**Fathomless Plunge (Level 14)**
Action: teleport yourself and up to five willing creatures within 30 ft. up to 1 mile to an
unoccupied space you can see next to a body of water at least as large as a pond. Once per short or
long rest.
