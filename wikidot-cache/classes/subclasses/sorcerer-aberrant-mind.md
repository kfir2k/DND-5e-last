## Class Features

**Psionic Spells (1st Level)**
Gain bonus spells at specific levels (1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th) that don't count against spells
known. Whenever you gain a sorcerer level, you can replace one spell you gained from this feature
with another spell of the same level. The new spell must be a divination or enchantment spell from
the sorcerer, warlock, or wizard spell list.

**Telepathic Speech (1st Level)**
As a bonus action, choose one creature you can see within 30 feet of you. Form a telepathic
connection lasting a number of minutes equal to your sorcerer level, with a range in miles equal to
your Charisma modifier (minimum 1 mile). Both parties must share a language they know mentally.

**Psionic Sorcery (6th Level)**
When you cast any spell of 1st level or higher gained from your Psionic Spells feature, you can
cast it by expending a spell slot as normal or by spending a number of sorcery points equal to the
spell's level. Casting via sorcery points requires no verbal, somatic, or material components
(except costly/consumed ones).

**Psychic Defenses (6th Level)**
You gain resistance to psychic damage, and you have advantage on saving throws against being
charmed or frightened.

**Revelation in Flesh (14th Level)**
As a bonus action, spend 1 or more sorcery points for a 10-minute transformation. Each point spent
grants one benefit: invisible creature detection within 60 feet, a hovering flying speed equal to
your walking speed, a swimming speed of twice your walking speed with underwater breathing, or
body pliability allowing movement through 1-inch spaces and escape from restraints.

**Warping Implosion (18th Level)**
As an action, teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within 120 feet. Creatures within 30 feet
of the space you left make a Strength save; failures take 3d10 force damage and are pulled toward
your former position, successes take half damage. Recharges on a long rest or for 5 sorcery points.
