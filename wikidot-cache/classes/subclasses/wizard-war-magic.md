## Class Features

**Arcane Deflection (2nd Level)**
Reaction, when you're hit by an attack or fail a saving throw: gain +2 AC against that attack, or
+4 to that save. If you do, you can't cast a spell (other than a cantrip) until the end of your next
turn.

**Tactical Wit (2nd Level)**
Add your Intelligence modifier to your initiative rolls.

**Power Surge (6th Level)**
Store magical energy called surges; you have a number of surges equal to your Intelligence modifier
(minimum 1), and you gain one surge whenever you finish a long rest. You gain another surge whenever
you successfully cast Counterspell or Dispel Magic, or when you end a short rest with no surges
stored. Once per turn when you deal damage with a spell, you can spend one surge to add force damage
equal to half your wizard level.

**Durable Magic (10th Level)**
While you're concentrating on a spell, you have a +2 bonus to AC and all saving throws.

**Deflecting Shroud (14th Level)**
Whenever you use Arcane Deflection, you can also deal force damage equal to half your wizard level
to up to three creatures of your choice within 60 feet of you.
