# Fighter: Echo Knight

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/fighter:echo-knight
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Features

**Manifest Echo (3rd level)**
"You can use a bonus action to magically manifest an echo of yourself in an unoccupied space you can see within 15 feet of you." Echo has AC 14 + your proficiency bonus, 1 hit point, and immunity to all conditions; it can move up to 30 ft per turn; you can teleport to swap places with it (costs movement equal to distance); you can make your attacks originate from either your space or the echo's space; and creatures moving 5+ ft while within the echo's reach provoke opportunity attacks from it.

**Unleash Incarnation (3rd level)**
"Whenever you take the Attack action, you can make one additional melee attack from the echo's position." Uses equal to Constitution modifier (minimum once), regained on a long rest.

**Echo Avatar (7th level)**
"As an action, you can see through your echo's eyes and hear through its ears," for up to 10 minutes; while doing so you are deafened and blinded with respect to your own senses. While using this, the echo can exist up to 1,000 feet away without being destroyed.

**Shadow Martyr (10th level)**
"Before the attack roll is made, you can use your reaction to teleport the echo to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the targeted creature," and the attack targets your echo instead. Usable once per short or long rest.

**Reclaim Potential (15th level)**
When your echo is destroyed, you gain temporary hit points equal to 2d6 + your Constitution modifier, if you don't already have temporary hit points. Uses equal to Constitution modifier (minimum once).

**Legion of One (18th level)**
You can have two echoes manifested simultaneously (manifesting a third destroys the two oldest); you can attack from either echo's position or your own. When you roll initiative and have no uses of Unleash Incarnation remaining, you regain one use.
