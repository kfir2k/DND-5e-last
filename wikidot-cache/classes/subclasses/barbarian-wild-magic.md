# Path of Wild Magic (Barbarian)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/barbarian:wild-magic (colon replaced with a dash in filename)
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Class Features

**Magic Awareness (3rd Level)**
Action: open your awareness to the presence of magic. Until the end of your next turn, you know the
location of any spell or magic item within 60 feet of you that isn't behind total cover. Uses =
proficiency bonus per long rest.

**Wild Surge (3rd Level)**
Upon entering rage, roll on the Wild Magic Surge table (d8). Any save required is DC 8 + proficiency
+ CON mod. Table summary:
1. 1d12 necrotic damage to chosen creatures within 30 ft.; you gain 1d12 temp HP.
2. Teleport up to 30 ft.; usable again as a bonus action each turn for the rest of the rage.
3. A spectral spirit appears near a chosen creature within 30 ft. and explodes for 1d6 force damage
   in a 5-ft. radius.
4. One weapon you're holding deals force damage instead of its normal type and gains the light and
   thrown (20/60) properties until the rage ends.
5. Attackers who hit you take 1d6 force damage while you're raging.
6. You gain +1 AC; allies within 10 ft. gain the same bonus until the rage ends.
7. The ground within 15 ft. of you becomes difficult terrain for enemies until the rage ends.
8. A bolt of light shoots from your chest: you take 1d6 radiant damage and are blinded until the
   start of your next turn.

**Bolstering Magic (6th Level)**
Touch one creature (including yourself): it rolls a d3 and either adds it as a bonus to its next
attack roll or ability check made within the next minute, or regains one expended spell slot of a
level no higher than the number rolled. Uses = proficiency bonus per long rest.

**Unstable Backlash (10th Level)**
Reaction, when you take damage or fail a saving throw while raging: roll on the Wild Magic Surge
table and replace the current active surge effect with the new roll.

**Controlled Surge (14th Level)**
Whenever you roll on the Wild Magic Surge table, roll twice and choose which of the two results to
apply (or apply both if they're the same result).
