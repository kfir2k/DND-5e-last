## Class Features

**Enchantment Savant (2nd Level)**
The gold and time you must spend to copy an enchantment spell into your spellbook is halved.

**Hypnotic Gaze (2nd Level)**
As an action, choose one creature within 5 feet that can see and hear you; it must succeed on a
Wisdom save (vs. your spell save DC) or be charmed until the end of your next turn — while charmed,
its speed is 0 and it is incapacitated. On each of your turns, you can use an action to maintain
this effect on the same target, extending it until the end of your next turn, out to any range as
long as you can see it. The effect ends if you move away from it, if it can no longer see or hear
you, or if it takes damage. On a successful save, or once the effect ends, you can't use this
feature on that creature again until you finish a long rest.

**Instinctive Charm (6th Level)**
Reaction, when a creature within 30 feet you can see attacks you: you can divert the attack, provided
another creature is within the attack's range. The attacker must make a Wisdom save (vs. your spell
save DC); on a fail, it must target the creature nearest to it other than you or itself. On a
success, you can't use this feature on that attacker again until you finish a long rest. A creature
that can't be charmed is immune.

**Split Enchantment (10th Level)**
When you cast an enchantment spell of 1st level or higher that targets only one creature, you can
have it target a second creature.

**Alter Memories (14th Level)**
When you cast an enchantment spell to charm one or more creatures, you can make one of them unaware
that it was charmed. Additionally, when the effect ends, you can use your action to make the
creature forget some of the time it was charmed — up to a number of hours equal to 1 + your Charisma
modifier (minimum 1), never more than the duration of the charm — if it fails an Intelligence save
against your spell save DC.
