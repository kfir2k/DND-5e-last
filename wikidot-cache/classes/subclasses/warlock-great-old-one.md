# The Great Old One (Warlock subclass)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/warlock:great-old-one
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Expanded Spell List

1st: Dissonant Whispers, Tasha's Hideous Laughter. 2nd: Detect Thoughts, Phantasmal Force. 3rd:
Clairvoyance, Sending. 4th: Dominate Beast, Evard's Black Tentacles. 5th: Dominate Person,
Telekinesis.

## Class Features

**Awakened Mind (Level 1)**
Telepathically communicate with any creature you can see within 30 ft.; the creature understands
you only if it knows at least one language.

**Entropic Ward (Level 6)**
Reaction when a creature attacks you: impose disadvantage on that attack roll. If it misses, your
next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn has advantage. Once per
short or long rest.

**Thought Shield (Level 10)**
Your thoughts can't be read by telepathy or magic unless you allow it; resistance to psychic
damage; when a creature deals psychic damage to you, it takes the same amount of damage back.

**Create Thrall (Level 14)**
Action: touch an incapacitated humanoid to charm it (no save). While charmed this way, it's
telepathically linked to you across any distance on the same plane. The charm lasts until you use
this feature again or a Remove Curse (or similar) is cast on the creature.
