## Features by level

**Level 3 — Rite of the Dawn**
When you activate a Crimson Rite, its extra damage becomes radiant instead of its usual type. The
weapon sheds bright light in a 20-ft. radius, you gain resistance to necrotic damage, and you roll
an extra hemocraft die when the rite hits an undead creature.

**Level 3 — Curse Specialist**
Gain one additional use of Blood Maledict. Your blood curses now affect any creature regardless of
whether it has blood (normally bloodless creatures are immune unless the curse is amplified).

**Level 7 — Aether Walk**
At the start of your turn, step partway into the Ethereal Plane for a number of rounds equal to
your hemocraft modifier (minimum 1): you can move through creatures/objects as difficult terrain
and see/affect creatures on the Ethereal Plane. Ending your turn inside an object deals you 1d10
force damage. Recharges after a short or long rest; two uses between rests at 15th level.

**Level 11 — Brand of Sundering**
Hits with an active Crimson Rite weapon deal an extra hemocraft die of damage. A creature branded
by you that has Incorporeal Movement can't use it to pass through creatures or objects.

**Level 15 — Blood Curse of the Exorcist**
Gain the Blood Curse of the Exorcist for Blood Maledict (doesn't count against curses known) — a
curse aimed at undead and fiends.

**Level 18 — Rite Revival**
If you have an active Crimson Rite and would be reduced to 0 HP without dying outright, you can
end all active rites to drop to 1 HP instead.
