## Class Features

**Level 3 - Guiding Whispers**
Learn the Guidance cantrip (doesn't count against cantrips known); for you it has a range of 60 ft.

**Level 3 - Spiritual Focus**
You can use an object such as a candle, crystal ball, skull, spirit board, or tarokka deck as a
spellcasting focus for your bard spells. Starting at 6th level, when a bard spell cast through it
deals damage or restores HP, roll a d6 and add the result to one damage or healing roll of the spell.

**Level 3 - Tales from Beyond**
Bonus action, while holding your Spiritual Focus: expend one use of Bardic Inspiration and roll on
the Spirit Tales table (using the Bardic Inspiration die) to determine a tale. As an action, choose
one creature within 30 ft. (can be yourself) as its target. Only one tale can be active at a time —
rolling again ends the previous one.

**Level 6 - Spirit Session**
Hour-long ritual (can be done during a short or long rest) using your Spiritual Focus with willing
creatures up to your proficiency bonus (including yourself); together you cast a Divination or
Necromancy spell of a level you can cast, no higher than the number of participants.

**Level 14 - Mystical Connection**
When you roll on the Spirit Tales table, roll the die twice and choose which result to use (or, on
a double, ignore the roll and choose any effect on the table).
