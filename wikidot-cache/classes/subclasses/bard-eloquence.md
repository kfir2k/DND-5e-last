## Class Features

**Level 3 - Silver Tongue**
When you make a Charisma (Persuasion) or Charisma (Deception) check, you can treat a d20 roll of
9 or lower as a 10.

**Level 3 - Unsettling Words**
Bonus action: expend one Bardic Inspiration use and target a creature within 60 ft. Roll the
Bardic Inspiration die; that creature subtracts the result from the next saving throw it makes
before the start of your next turn.

**Level 6 - Unfailing Inspiration**
When a creature adds one of your Bardic Inspiration dice to an ability check, attack roll, or
saving throw and the roll still fails, the creature keeps the die instead of expending it.

**Level 6 - Universal Speech**
Action: choose a number of creatures within 60 ft. up to your CHA modifier; they magically
understand your speech regardless of language for 1 hour. Recharges on a long rest, or you can
expend a spell slot to use it again.

**Level 14 - Infectious Inspiration**
Reaction, when a creature within 60 ft. adds your Bardic Inspiration die to a roll that succeeds:
grant a different creature within 60 ft. a Bardic Inspiration die at no cost. Uses equal to your
CHA modifier (minimum 1), regained on a long rest.
