# Path of the Ancestral Guardian (Barbarian)

Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Class Features

**Ancestral Protectors (3rd Level)**
Spectral warriors appear when you enter your rage. While you're raging, the first creature you hit
with an attack on your turn becomes the target of the warriors, which hinder its attacks. The
target gains disadvantage on attack rolls not directed at you until your next turn starts. When the
target hits another creature, that creature gains resistance to the damage. This effect ends if
your rage ends.

**Spirit Shield (6th Level)**
If you are raging and another creature you can see within 30 feet of you takes damage, you can use
your reaction to reduce that damage by 2d6. The reduction increases to 3d6 at 10th level and 4d6 at
14th level.

**Consult the Spirits (10th Level)**
You may cast Augury or Clairvoyance without expending spell slots or material components. For
Clairvoyance, an ancestral spirit replaces the spherical sensor. Wisdom determines your spellcasting
ability. You regain this capability after completing a short or long rest.

**Vengeful Ancestors (14th Level)**
When you use your Spirit Shield to reduce the damage of an attack, the attacker takes an amount of
force damage equal to the amount your Spirit Shield prevented.
