## Class Features

**Level 3 - Bonus Proficiencies**
Proficiency with medium armor and the scimitar. A simple or martial melee weapon you're
proficient with can be used as a spellcasting focus for your bard spells.

**Level 3 - Fighting Style**
Choose Dueling (+2 damage with a melee weapon in one hand, nothing in the other) or Two-Weapon
Fighting (add your ability modifier to the damage of the second attack).

**Level 3 - Blade Flourish**
When you take the Attack action, your walking speed increases by 10 ft. until the end of the
turn, and once per turn when you hit with a weapon attack you can expend one Bardic Inspiration
use to add the die to the damage and apply one flourish: Defensive (also add the die to your AC
until your next turn), Slashing (also deal the extra damage to one other creature within 5 ft. of
the target), or Mobile (also push the target up to 5 ft. plus the die roll, then move up to your
walking speed without provoking opportunity attacks).

**Level 6 - Extra Attack**
Attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action.

**Level 14 - Master's Flourish**
When you use Blade Flourish, you can roll a d6 and use it instead of expending a Bardic
Inspiration die.
