# School of Divination (Wizard)

Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Class Features

**Divination Savant (2nd Level)**
The gold and time you must spend to copy a divination spell into your spellbook is halved.

**Portent (2nd Level)**
When you finish a long rest, roll two d20s and record the numbers. You can replace any attack roll,
saving throw, or ability check made by you or a creature you can see with one of these foretelling
rolls, choosing to do so before the roll is made. Each foretelling roll can only be used once, and
you can use only one foretelling roll per roll. Unused rolls are lost when you finish your next long
rest.

**Expert Divination (6th Level)**
Casting divination spells comes at a reduced cost to you. When you cast a divination spell of 2nd
level or higher using a spell slot, you regain one expended spell slot of a lower level (maximum
5th).

**The Third Eye (10th Level)**
As an action, you gain one of the following benefits of your choice until it ends (no concentration
required): Darkvision out to 60 feet; the ability to read any language (Greater Comprehension);
Ethereal Sight (see into the Ethereal Plane out to 60 feet); or See Invisibility (see invisible
creatures/objects within 10 feet that are within line of sight). The benefit lasts until you're
incapacitated or you take a short or long rest, after which you can use it again.

**Greater Portent (14th Level)**
Your Portent feature grows more powerful: you roll three d20s for it instead of two after each long
rest.
