# Artificer: Artillerist

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/artificer:artillerist
Fetched: 2026-11-17 (HTTP 200, read in full; no (UA) markers)
Book: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything / Eberron: Rising from the Last War

> **Paraphrased, not verbatim** (fetch tool quote limit). Full coverage of every feature and number.

- **Tool Proficiency (3rd):** woodcarver's tools; if already proficient, one other artisan's tool.
- **Artillerist Spells (3rd):** always prepared, don't count against prepared spells. 3rd Shield, Thunderwave
  · 5th Scorching Ray, Shatter · 9th Fireball, Wind Wall · 13th Ice Storm, Wall of Fire · 17th Cone of Cold,
  Wall of Force.
- **Eldritch Cannon (3rd):** action: create a Small or Tiny cannon in an unoccupied space on a horizontal
  surface within 5 ft. (Small occupies its space; Tiny can be held in one hand). Once per long rest, or by
  expending a spell slot; only one at a time. Magical object: AC 18, HP 5 × artificer level, immune to poison
  and psychic damage, all ability scores 10 (+0); mending restores 2d6 HP; disappears at 0 HP or after 1 hour;
  dismiss as an action. Bonus action: activate it within 60 ft.; with legs, the same bonus action moves it up
  to 15 ft. Types:
  - Flamethrower: 15-ft. cone, DEX save vs. your spell save DC, 2d8 fire (half on success); ignites flammable
    unattended objects.
  - Force Ballista: ranged spell attack from the cannon at a target within 120 ft.; 2d8 force, and a creature is
    pushed up to 5 ft. away.
  - Protector: the cannon and each creature you choose within 10 ft. gain 1d8 + INT mod (minimum +1) temp HP.
- **Arcane Firearm (5th):** after a long rest, carve sigils into a wand, staff or rod with woodcarver's tools;
  it's a spellcasting focus for artificer spells, and an artificer spell cast through it adds 1d8 to one of the
  spell's damage rolls. Sigils move if carved into another item.
- **Explosive Cannon (9th):** cannon damage rolls +1d8. Action: detonate a cannon within 60 ft. — destroys it;
  each creature within 20 ft. makes a DEX save vs. your spell save DC, 3d8 force (half on success).
- **Fortified Position (15th):** you and allies have half cover within 10 ft. of a cannon you created; you can
  have two cannons at once — create both with one action (one spell slot), activate both with one bonus action.
