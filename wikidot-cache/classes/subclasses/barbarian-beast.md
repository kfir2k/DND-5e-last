# Path of the Beast (Barbarian)

## Class Features

**Form of the Beast (3rd Level)**
When you enter your rage, you can transform, revealing the bestial power within you. Choose one
natural weapon each time (usable while raging):
- **Bite**: 1d8 piercing damage. Once on each of your turns when you damage a creature with this
  bite, you regain HP equal to your proficiency bonus, provided you have less than half your hit
  points when you hit.
- **Claws**: 1d6 slashing damage. Once on each of your turns when you attack with a claw using the
  Attack action, you can make one additional claw attack.
- **Tail**: 1d8 piercing damage, reach property. If a creature you can see within 10 feet of you
  hits you with an attack roll, you can use your reaction to swipe your tail and roll a d8, applying
  the result as a bonus to your AC against that attack.

**Bestial Soul (6th Level)**
Your natural weapons count as magical for overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks
and damage. After completing a rest, choose one benefit lasting until your next rest: a swimming
speed, a climbing speed, or enhanced jump distance via Strength checks.

**Infectious Fury (10th Level)**
When you hit a creature with your natural weapons while raging, the target makes a Wisdom saving
throw (DC 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus) or suffers either a psychic
effect that forces it to make a melee attack against a creature of your choice, or 2d12 psychic
damage (your choice). Uses = proficiency bonus per long rest.

**Call the Hunt (14th Level)**
When you enter your rage, choose willing creatures within 30 feet (max = your Constitution
modifier). You gain 5 temporary hit points for each creature that accepts. Each chosen creature
gains bonus damage once per turn: roll a d6 and add the result to one damage roll. Uses =
proficiency bonus per long rest.
