# Divine Soul (Sorcerer)

Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Class Features

**Divine Magic (1st Level)**
You can choose sorcerer spells from either the cleric or sorcerer spell list. Additionally, choose
an affinity for the source of your divine power: good, evil, law, chaos, or neutrality. You gain
one bonus spell based on affinity: Cure Wounds (good), Inflict Wounds (evil), Bless (law), Bane
(chaos), or Protection from Evil and Good (neutrality). This bonus spell doesn't count against
spells known.

**Favored by the Gods (1st Level)**
When you fail a saving throw or miss with an attack roll, you may roll 2d4 and add it to the total,
possibly changing the outcome. This feature recharges after a short or long rest.

**Empowered Healing (6th Level)**
When you or an ally within 5 feet rolls dice for healing from a spell, you can spend 1 sorcery
point to reroll any number of those dice once, provided you aren't incapacitated. Limited to once
per turn.

**Angelic Form (14th Level)**
As a bonus action, manifest spectral wings granting 30 feet of flying speed. The wings persist
until you become incapacitated, die, or dismiss them. Wing appearance depends on affinity: eagle
(good/law), bat (evil/chaos), or dragonfly (neutrality).

**Unearthly Recovery (18th Level)**
As a bonus action when below half your hit points, regain a number of hit points equal to half your
hit point maximum. Recharges after a long rest.
