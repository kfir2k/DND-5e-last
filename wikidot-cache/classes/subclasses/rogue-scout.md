Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Skirmisher (3rd level)
"You can move up to half your speed as a reaction when an enemy ends its turn within 5 feet of you.
This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks."

## Survivalist (3rd level)
Gain proficiency in the Nature and Survival skills if you lack them. Your proficiency bonus is
doubled for ability checks using either skill.

## Superior Mobility (9th level)
"Your walking speed increases by 10 feet. If you have a climbing or swimming speed, this increase
applies to that speed as well."

## Ambush Master (13th level)
You gain advantage on initiative rolls. Additionally, the first creature you hit in the first round
of combat becomes vulnerable to attacks: "attack rolls against that target have advantage until the
start of your next turn."

## Sudden Strike (17th level)
When taking the Attack action, make one additional attack as part of it. This attack can benefit
from Sneak Attack even if you've already used it this turn, but you can't apply Sneak Attack against
the same target more than once per turn.
