## Extracted content used

**Path of the Kensei (3rd level)**
- Kensei Weapons: choose two weapon types as kensei weapons, one melee and one ranged (no heavy or special properties; longbow is valid). You gain proficiency with them and they count as monk weapons for you. Choose an additional kensei weapon at 6th, 11th, and 17th level.
- Agile Parry: after making an unarmed strike during your Attack action while holding a melee kensei weapon, gain +2 AC until the start of your next turn while that weapon is in hand.
- Kensei's Shot: bonus action to empower ranged kensei attacks, adding an extra 1d4 damage of the weapon's type until the end of the turn.
- Way of the Brush: proficiency with calligrapher's supplies or painter's supplies.

**One with the Blade (6th level)**
- Magic Kensei Weapons: your attacks with kensei weapons count as magical for overcoming resistance/immunity to nonmagical attacks.
- Deft Strike: once per turn on a hit, spend 1 ki point to deal extra damage to the target equal to your Martial Arts die.

**Sharpen the Blade (11th level)**
Bonus action: spend up to 3 ki points to grant a kensei weapon you're holding a bonus to attack and damage rolls equal to the ki spent, lasting 1 minute.

**Unerring Accuracy (17th level)**
If you miss with an attack roll using a monk weapon, you can reroll it. Usable only once on each of your turns.
