## Class Features

**Star Map (Level 2)**
Create a Tiny object (your star map) that acts as a spellcasting focus for your druid spells and
lets you re-create the map for free if lost. You learn the Guidance cantrip and always have Guiding
Bolt prepared (doesn't count against prepared spells); you can cast Guiding Bolt without a spell
slot a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all uses on a long rest.

**Starry Form (Level 2)**
Bonus action: expend a Wild Shape use to assume a starry form for 10 minutes (constellations of
light along your joints; shed dim light 10 ft.) instead of a beast shape. Choose one constellation,
usable once per turn while in the form:

- **Archer:** bonus action ranged spell attack against a target within 60 ft. for 1d8 + WIS mod
  radiant damage.
- **Chalice:** when you cast a spell that restores hit points, one target of the spell (you or an
  ally within 30 ft.) regains an extra 1d8 + WIS mod hit points.
- **Dragon:** you and creatures within 30 ft. treat any Intelligence or Wisdom check, or
  Constitution save, that rolls 9 or lower as a 10.

**Cosmic Omen (Level 6)**
After a long rest, roll a die: even = Weal, odd = Woe. A number of times equal to your proficiency
bonus (until your next long rest), use your reaction when a creature within 30 ft. makes an attack
roll, saving throw, or ability check to add (Weal) or subtract (Woe) 1d6 from the roll, after
seeing it but before the result is applied.

**Twinkling Constellations (Level 10)**
Archer's and Chalice's dice increase to 2d8; Dragon's form grants a 20-foot flying speed and the
ability to hover; you can also change which constellation is active at the start of each of your
turns while in Starry Form.

**Full of Stars (Level 14)**
While in Starry Form, you have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.
