# The Genie (Warlock subclass)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/warlock:the-genie
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Expanded Spell List

Grants genie-themed spells plus kind-specific options across levels 1-5, and Wish at 9th level to
all genie kinds.

## Class Features

**Genie's Vessel / Genie Kind (Level 1)**
Choose a genie kind: Dao (earth), Djinni (air), Efreeti (fire), or Marid (water) — this determines
the damage type below and flavors the vessel. Your vessel is a Tiny object that serves as your
spellcasting focus.

- *Bottled Respite*: you can enter the vessel's extradimensional interior (a 20-ft.-radius cylinder)
  and remain for up to twice your proficiency bonus in hours; you can bring up to five other
  creatures with you (unlocked further at level 10). Leave via bonus action or if the vessel is
  destroyed. Recharges after a long rest.
- *Genie's Wrath*: once per turn on a hit, deal extra damage equal to your proficiency bonus —
  bludgeoning (Dao), thunder (Djinni), fire (Efreeti), or cold (Marid).

Vessel AC = your spell save DC; vessel HP = your warlock level + proficiency bonus.

**Elemental Gift (Level 6)**
Resistance to the damage type tied to your genie kind. Bonus action: gain a flying speed of 30 ft.
and hover, for 10 minutes. Uses equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

**Sanctuary Vessel (Level 10)**
Up to five willing creatures within 30 ft. can enter your vessel with you. Anyone who spends 10+
minutes inside gains the benefit of a short rest and adds your proficiency bonus to Hit Dice they
spend while there.

**Limited Wish (Level 14)**
Once, duplicate the effect of any spell of 6th level or lower without needing to meet its
requirements (as an action). Recharges after 1d4 long rests.
