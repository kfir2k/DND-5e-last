# School of Necromancy (Wizard)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/wizard:necromancy (colon replaced with a dash in filename)
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Class Features

**Necromancy Savant (2nd Level)**
The gold and time you must spend to copy a necromancy spell into your spellbook is halved.

**Grim Harvest (2nd Level)**
Once per turn, when you kill one or more creatures with a spell of 1st level or higher, you regain
HP equal to twice the spell's level (three times its level instead if it's a necromancy spell). No
benefit from killing constructs or undead.

**Undead Thralls (6th Level)**
You add Animate Dead to your spellbook if it isn't there already. When you cast Animate Dead, you
can target one additional corpse or pile of bones, creating another zombie or skeleton. Whenever
you create an undead with a necromancy spell, it has additional maximum (and current) HP equal to
your wizard level, and it adds your proficiency bonus to its weapon damage rolls.

**Inured to Undeath (10th Level)**
You have resistance to necrotic damage, and your hit point maximum can't be reduced.

**Command Undead (14th Level)**
As an action, choose one undead you can see within 60 feet; it must make a Charisma save against
your wizard spell save DC. On a fail, it falls under your control (obeying your commands) until you
lose control of it; on a success, you can't use this on it again. Intelligent undead get advantage
on the save; if their Intelligence is 12 or higher, they can repeat the save once per hour to break
free.
