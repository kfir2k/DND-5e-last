# Oath of the Ancients (Paladin)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/paladin:ancients (colon replaced with a dash in filename per this folder's README)
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Tenets
Kindle the Light (mercy and kindness), Shelter the Light (oppose wickedness), Preserve Your Own
Light (delight in beauty), Be the Light (a beacon of hope).

## Oath Spells
- 3rd: Ensnaring Strike, Speak with Animals
- 5th: Moonbeam, Misty Step
- 9th: Plant Growth, Protection from Energy
- 13th: Ice Storm, Stoneskin
- 17th: Commune with Nature, Tree Stride

## Channel Divinity Options (3rd level)
**Nature's Wrath:** Action — spectral vines spring up and reach for a creature within 10 feet,
which must make a Strength or Dexterity save (its choice) or be restrained. It repeats the save
at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

**Turn the Faithless:** Action, present holy symbol — fey and fiends within 30 feet that can see
or hear you make a Wisdom save or are turned for 1 minute or until they take damage. Turned
creatures must spend their turns trying to move away from you, can't willingly move within 30 ft.
of you, can't take reactions, and on their turn can only Dash or try to escape an effect that
prevents movement.

## Aura of Warding (7th level)
You and friendly creatures within 10 feet of you have resistance to damage from spells. Range
increases to 30 feet at 18th level (Aura Improvements).

## Undying Sentinel (15th level)
When reduced to 0 hit points and not killed outright, you can choose to drop to 1 hit point
instead. Once you use this, you can't again until you finish a long rest. You also suffer none of
the drawbacks of old age and can't be aged magically.

## Elder Champion (20th level)
Action: undergo a transformation for 1 minute, gaining: regain 10 HP at the start of each of your
turns; you can cast a paladin spell with a casting time of 1 action as a bonus action instead;
enemy creatures within 10 feet of you have disadvantage on saving throws against your paladin
spells and Channel Divinity options. Once per long rest.
