# Oath of Glory (Paladin)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/paladin:glory (colon replaced with a dash in filename per this folder's README)
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Oath Spells
- 3rd: Guiding Bolt, Heroism
- 5th: Enhance Ability, Magic Weapon
- 9th: Haste, Protection from Energy
- 13th: Compulsion, Freedom of Movement
- 17th: Commune, Flame Strike

## Channel Divinity Options (3rd level)
**Peerless Athlete:** Channel Divinity — for 10 minutes, gain advantage on Strength (Athletics)
and Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks; your carrying capacity doubles and your jumping distance
increases by 10 feet.

**Inspiring Smite:** Right after you deal damage with Divine Smite, use Channel Divinity to
distribute 2d8 + your paladin level in temporary hit points among allies within 30 feet (your
choice how it's split).

## Aura of Alacrity (7th level)
Your walking speed increases by 10 feet. If you aren't incapacitated, an ally who starts their
turn within 5 feet of you (10 feet at 18th level) also gains +10 ft. speed until the end of that
turn.

## Glorious Defense (15th level)
Reaction: when an ally you can see within 5 feet is hit by an attack, grant it a bonus to AC equal
to your Charisma modifier (minimum +1), possibly causing the attack to miss; if it misses, you can
make one weapon attack against the attacker. Usable a number of times equal to your Charisma
modifier (minimum once), regaining all uses on a long rest.

## Living Legend (20th level)
Bonus action: for 1 minute, gain advantage on Charisma checks, once per turn turn a missed weapon
attack into a hit, and reroll any failed saving throw you make (must use the new roll). Can't use
again until a long rest, unless you expend a 5th-level spell slot to use it again.
