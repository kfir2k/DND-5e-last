# Bladesinging (Wizard)

## Class Features

**Training in War and Song (2nd Level)**
You gain proficiency with light armor and with one type of one-handed melee weapon of your choice.
You also gain proficiency in the Performance skill if you don't already have it.

**Bladesong (2nd Level)**
As a bonus action, activate Bladesong (as long as you're not wearing medium/heavy armor or a shield)
for 1 minute or until you're incapacitated, don medium/heavy armor or a shield, or use two hands to
make an attack with a weapon. Uses per long rest = your proficiency bonus. While active: +AC equal
to your Intelligence modifier (min +1); +10 ft. walking speed; advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics)
checks; and a bonus equal to your Intelligence modifier (min +1) to Constitution saves made to
maintain concentration on a spell.

**Extra Attack (6th Level)**
You can attack twice, instead of once, when you take the Attack action; you may replace one of the
attacks with a cantrip.

**Song of Defense (10th Level)**
Reaction, when you take damage while Bladesong is active: expend a spell slot to reduce the damage
to yourself by an amount equal to 5 times the slot's level.

**Song of Victory (14th Level)**
While Bladesong is active, add your Intelligence modifier (min +1) to the damage of your melee
weapon attacks.
