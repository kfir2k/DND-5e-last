# Oath of Vengeance (Paladin)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/paladin:vengeance (colon replaced with a dash in filename per this folder's README)
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Oath Spells
- 3rd: Bane, Hunter's Mark
- 5th: Hold Person, Misty Step
- 9th: Haste, Protection from Energy
- 13th: Banishment, Dimension Door
- 17th: Hold Monster, Scrying

## Channel Divinity Options (3rd level)
**Abjure Enemy:** Action, present holy symbol, Channel Divinity — target one creature within 60
feet that can see/hear you (fiends and undead have disadvantage on the save) for a Wisdom save. On
a fail: frightened for 1 minute or until it takes damage, speed becomes 0, can't benefit from
speed bonuses. On a success: its speed is halved for 1 minute or until it takes damage.

**Vow of Enmity:** Bonus action, Channel Divinity — target one creature you can see within 10
feet: you have advantage on attack rolls against it for 1 minute or until it drops to 0 HP or
falls unconscious.

## Relentless Avenger (7th level)
When you hit a creature with an opportunity attack, you can move up to half your speed immediately
after the attack as part of the same reaction, without provoking opportunity attacks.

## Soul of Vengeance (15th level)
Reaction: when a creature affected by your Vow of Enmity makes an attack, you can make a melee
weapon attack against it if it's within your reach, provided you can see it.

## Avenging Angel (20th level)
Action: transform for 1 hour, gaining a 60-foot flying speed and an aura of menace out to 30 feet
— enemies that start their turn there must succeed on a Wisdom save or become frightened of you
for 1 minute. You have advantage on attack rolls against frightened creatures. Once per long rest.
