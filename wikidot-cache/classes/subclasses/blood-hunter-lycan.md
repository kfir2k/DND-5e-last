## Features by level

**Level 3 — Heightened Senses**
Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.

**Level 3 — Hybrid Transformation**
Bonus action: transform into a bestial hybrid form for up to 1 hour, once per short/long rest.
Can speak, use equipment, wear armor; revert as a bonus action (or automatically at 0 HP/dead).
While transformed: advantage on Strength checks/saves; +1 melee damage bonus (+2 at 11th, +3 at
18th); resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning/piercing/slashing damage (not silvered); +1 AC
without heavy armor; Crimson Rite can apply to your unarmed strikes (1d6, 1d8 at 11th) using
Strength or Dexterity; an extra unarmed strike as part of your Attack action. Bloodlust: dropping
below half HP forces a DC 8 WIS save or you must move toward and attack the nearest creature (auto-
fails while concentrating or raging).

**Level 7 — Stalker's Prowess**
+10 ft. speed; +10 ft. long jump, +3 ft. high jump. Unarmed strikes gain a growing bonus to
attack rolls (+1, +2 at 11th, +3 at 18th). Crimson Rites active on your unarmed strikes count as
magical.

**Level 11 — Advanced Transformation**
Hybrid Transformation now recharges twice per short/long rest. Lycan Regeneration: while below
half HP and in hybrid form, regain 1 + CON modifier (minimum 1) HP at the start of each of your
turns.

**Level 15 — Brand of the Voracious**
Advantage on the Bloodlust saving throw while transformed, and advantage on attack rolls against
creatures you've branded while transformed.

**Level 18 — Hybrid Transformation Mastery**
Transform an unlimited number of times, and the form no longer expires after 1 hour — it lasts
until you choose to revert.

**Level 18 — Blood Curse of the Howl**
Gain the Blood Curse of the Howl for Blood Maledict (doesn't count against curses known) — a
terrifying howl that can frighten creatures near you.
