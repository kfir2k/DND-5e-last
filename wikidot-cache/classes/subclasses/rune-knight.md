# Fighter: Rune Knight

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/fighter:rune-knight
Fetched: 2026-09-05 (Stone Rune / Storm Rune verbatim text added 2026-09-11)

## Features

**Bonus Proficiencies (3rd level)**
"You gain proficiency with smith's tools, and you learn to speak, read, and write Giant."

**Rune Carver (3rd level)**
You learn two runes of your choice at 3rd level (3 known at 7th, 4 at 10th, 5 at 15th level), inscribing them onto your armor, weapons, or shield after a long rest. Hill Rune and Storm Rune both require 7th level or higher to choose. Rune Magic save DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + Constitution modifier.

Rune options:
- **Cloud Rune**: Advantage on Sleight of Hand and Deception checks. Reaction: when you or a creature within 30 ft you can see is hit by an attack roll, invoke the rune to redirect the attack to a different creature within 30 feet (other than the attacker), using the same roll. Once per short/long rest.
- **Fire Rune**: Doubles proficiency bonus on tool checks. On a weapon hit, invoke to deal extra 2d6 fire damage and force a Strength save or the target is restrained for 1 minute, taking 2d6 fire damage at the start of each of its turns while restrained. Once per short/long rest.
- **Frost Rune**: Advantage on Animal Handling and Intimidation checks. Bonus action: invoke for a +2 bonus to all ability checks and saving throws using Strength or Constitution, for 10 minutes. Once per short/long rest.
- **Stone Rune**: Advantage on Insight checks; 120 ft darkvision. Reaction when a creature you can see ends its turn within 30 ft of you: it makes a Wisdom save or is charmed by you for 1 minute, with speed 0 and incapacitated while charmed (repeats the save at the end of each of its turns). Once per short/long rest.
- **Hill Rune** (7th level or higher): Advantage on saves against poison; resistance to poison damage. Bonus action: invoke for resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage for 1 minute. Once per short/long rest.
- **Storm Rune** (7th level or higher): Advantage on Arcana checks; can't be surprised while conscious. Bonus action: invoke to enter a prophetic state for 1 minute or until incapacitated — while it lasts, reaction to give advantage or disadvantage on an attack roll, save, or ability check made by you or another creature you can see within 60 ft. Once per short/long rest.

Verbatim (Stone Rune): "...when a creature you can see ends its turn within 30 feet of you, you can use your reaction to invoke the rune and force the creature to make a Wisdom saving throw. Unless the save succeeds, the creature is charmed by you for 1 minute. While charmed in this way, the creature has a speed of 0 and is incapacitated, descending into a dreamy stupor. The creature repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success."

Verbatim (Storm Rune): "...you can invoke the rune as a bonus action to enter a prophetic state for 1 minute or until you're incapacitated. Until the state ends, when you or another creature you can see within 60 feet of you makes an attack roll, a saving throw, or an ability check, you can use your reaction to cause the roll to have advantage or disadvantage."

**Giant's Might (3rd level)**
Bonus action: magically transform for 1 minute, becoming Large if you have space (equipment grows with you), gaining advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws, and once per turn one of your attacks with a weapon or unarmed strike deals an extra 1d6 damage. Number of uses equal to proficiency bonus, regained on long rest.

**Runic Shield (7th level)**
Reaction when an attack roll is made against a creature you can see within 60 feet of you: invoke a rune to force the attacker to reroll. Uses equal to proficiency bonus per long rest.

**Great Stature (10th level)**
You grow 3d4 inches in height, permanently. Giant's Might's extra damage increases to 1d8.

**Master of Runes (15th level)**
You can invoke each of your runes twice between rests instead of once (uses recharge on a short or long rest).

**Runic Juggernaut (18th level)**
Giant's Might's extra damage increases to 1d10, and while transformed you can become Huge instead of Large (if you have space), gaining an additional 5 feet of reach.
