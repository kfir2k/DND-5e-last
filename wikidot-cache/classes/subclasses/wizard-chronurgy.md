# Chronurgy Magic (Wizard)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/wizard:chronurgy (colon replaced with a dash in filename)
Fetched: 2026-09-05
Note: Explorer's Guide to Wildemount subclass.

## Class Features

**Chronal Shift (2nd Level)**
Reaction, after you or a creature you can see within 30 feet makes an attack roll, ability check, or
saving throw (before the result is known): force it to reroll. Two uses per long rest.

**Temporal Awareness (2nd Level)**
Add your Intelligence modifier to your initiative rolls.

**Momentary Stasis (6th Level)**
As an action, target a Large or smaller creature within 60 feet; it must succeed on a Constitution
save or be suspended in a bubble of frozen time (incapacitated, speed 0) until the end of your next
turn or until it takes damage. Uses per long rest equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1).

**Arcane Abeyance (10th Level)**
When you cast a spell of 4th level or lower, you can condense its magic into a gray mote inside a
tiny bead (AC 15, 1 HP) instead of having it take effect; the spell releases (targeting as originally
cast) when the bead is destroyed or a creature uses its action to break it, within 1 hour. Usable
once per short or long rest.

**Convergent Future (14th Level)**
Reaction, when you or a creature you can see within 60 feet makes an attack roll, ability check, or
saving throw: ignore the number rolled and decide whether it succeeded or failed by the minimum
margin. Each use of this feature costs you one level of exhaustion, removable only by finishing a
long rest.
