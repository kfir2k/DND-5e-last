# Ranger: Swarmkeeper

Fetched: 2026-09-05

## 3rd Level - Gathered Swarm

A swarm of intangible nature spirits bonds to you and assists in battle. Once per turn after hitting a creature with an attack, you can cause the swarm to: deal 1d6 piercing damage, force a Strength save to move the target 15 feet, or move yourself 5 feet.

## 3rd Level - Swarmkeeper Magic

Learn the Mage Hand cantrip (appearing as nature spirits). Additional bonus spells at specific levels: Faerie Fire (3rd), Web (5th), Gaseous Form (9th), Arcane Eye (13th), Insect Plague (17th). These count as ranger spells but don't count against your known spells.

## 7th Level - Writhing Tide

Bonus action: gain a flying speed of 10 feet and can hover, for 1 minute or until incapacitated. Uses equal to proficiency bonus, all regained on a long rest.

## 11th Level - Mighty Swarm

Gathered Swarm damage increases to 1d8. Failed saves against movement also knock creatures prone. When you're moved by the swarm, you gain half cover until the start of your next turn.

## 15th Level - Swarming Dispersal

Reaction when you take damage: gain resistance to that damage, vanish into your swarm, and teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within 30 feet. Uses equal to proficiency bonus, all regained on a long rest.
