## Class Features

**Conjuration Savant (2nd Level)**
The gold and time you must spend to copy a conjuration spell into your spellbook is halved.

**Minor Conjuration (2nd Level)**
As an action, you conjure up an inanimate object in your hand or on the ground within 10 feet that
is no larger than 3 feet on a side and weighs no more than 10 pounds. The object is visibly magical,
shedding dim light out to 5 feet. It vanishes after 1 hour, if it takes or deals any damage, or if
you use this feature again.

**Benign Transposition (6th Level)**
As an action, you can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. Alternatively, you
can swap places with a willing Small or Medium creature within range, teleporting it to the space
you left. You can use this feature once per long rest, or again by expending a spell slot of 1st
level or higher when you cast a conjuration spell.

**Focused Conjuration (10th Level)**
While you are concentrating on a conjuration spell, your concentration can't be broken as a result
of taking damage.

**Durable Summons (14th Level)**
Any creature that you summon or create with a conjuration spell has 30 temporary hit points.
