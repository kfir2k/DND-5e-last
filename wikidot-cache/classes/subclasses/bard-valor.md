## Class Features

**Level 3 - Bonus Proficiencies**
Proficiency with medium armor, shields, and martial weapons.

**Level 3 - Combat Inspiration**
A creature holding a Bardic Inspiration die you gave it can add the die to a weapon damage roll it
just made, or use its reaction to add the die to its AC against one attack (after seeing the roll
but before the outcome is known).

**Level 6 - Extra Attack**
Attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action.

**Level 14 - Battle Magic**
When you use your action to cast a bard spell, you can make one weapon attack as a bonus action.
