# Oath of Redemption (Paladin)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/paladin:redemption (colon replaced with a dash in filename per this folder's README)
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Oath Spells
- 3rd: Sanctuary, Sleep
- 5th: Calm Emotions, Hold Person
- 9th: Counterspell, Hypnotic Pattern
- 13th: Otiluke's Resilient Sphere, Stoneskin
- 17th: Hold Monster, Wall of Force

## Channel Divinity Options (3rd level)
**Emissary of Peace:** Bonus action, Channel Divinity — gain a +5 bonus to Charisma (Persuasion)
checks for the next 10 minutes.

**Rebuke the Violent:** Reaction, Channel Divinity — when a creature within 30 feet damages
another creature (other than you), force it to make a Wisdom save; on a fail it takes radiant
damage equal to the damage it just dealt, half as much on a success.

## Aura of the Guardian (7th level)
Reaction: when a creature within 10 feet of you (30 feet at 18th level) takes damage, you can
magically take that damage instead. It can't be reduced or prevented, and doesn't transfer any
other effects from the attack.

## Protective Spirit (15th level)
If you end your turn in combat with fewer than half your max HP remaining and aren't
incapacitated, regain 1d6 + half your paladin level in HP.

## Emissary of Redemption (20th level)
Gain resistance to all damage dealt by other creatures. Whenever a creature deals damage to you,
it takes radiant damage equal to half the damage you took (rounded down). These benefits stop
working against any creature you attack or damage (with a spell or otherwise) until you finish a
long rest.
