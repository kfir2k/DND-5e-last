# Oath of Conquest (Paladin)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/paladin:conquest (colon replaced with a dash in filename per this folder's README)
Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Oath Spells
- 3rd: Armor of Agathys, Command
- 5th: Hold Person, Spiritual Weapon
- 9th: Bestow Curse, Fear
- 13th: Dominate Beast, Stoneskin
- 17th: Cloudkill, Dominate Person

## Channel Divinity Options (3rd level)
**Conquering Presence:** Action — each creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet
makes a Wisdom save or is frightened for 1 minute, repeating the save at the end of each of its
turns.

**Guided Strike:** After you make an attack roll but before the DM says whether it hits or misses,
use Channel Divinity to gain a +10 bonus to the roll.

## Aura of Conquest (7th level)
Frightened creatures within 10 feet of you (30 feet at 18th level) have their speed reduced to 0,
and take psychic damage equal to half your paladin level (rounded down) whenever they start their
turn there.

## Scornful Rebuke (15th level)
While you aren't incapacitated, any creature that hits you with an attack takes psychic damage
equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of 1).

## Invincible Conqueror (20th level)
Action: transform for 1 minute, gaining resistance to all damage, one additional attack each time
you take the Attack action, and your weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.
Once per long rest.
