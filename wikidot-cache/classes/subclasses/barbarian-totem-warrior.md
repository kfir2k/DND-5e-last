# Path of the Totem Warrior (Barbarian)

Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Class Features

**Spirit Seeker (3rd Level)**
Gain the ability to cast Beast Sense and Speak with Animals, but only as rituals.

**Totem Spirit (3rd Level)**
Choose a totem animal and acquire a physical totem object; benefit lasts while raging (unless
noted):
- **Bear**: resistance to all damage except psychic damage.
- **Eagle**: other creatures have disadvantage on opportunity attacks against you, and you can Dash
  as a bonus action (requires not wearing heavy armor).
- **Elk**: walking speed increases by 15 feet (requires not wearing heavy armor).
- **Tiger**: +10 ft. to your long jump distance and +3 ft. to your high jump distance.
- **Wolf**: friends have advantage on melee attack rolls against any creature within 5 ft. of you
  that is hostile to you.

**Aspect of the Beast (6th Level)** — permanent benefit tied to your totem animal:
- **Bear**: carrying capacity doubled; advantage on STR checks to push/pull/lift/break objects.
- **Eagle**: see up to 1 mile with no difficulty; dim light doesn't impose disadvantage on
  Perception checks.
- **Elk**: travel pace doubled for up to ten companions within 60 ft. of you.
- **Tiger**: proficiency in two of Athletics, Acrobatics, Stealth, Survival.
- **Wolf**: track creatures at a fast travel pace, and move stealthily at a normal pace.

**Spirit Walker (10th Level)**
Cast Commune with Nature as a ritual; a spiritual version of your totem animal appears to you and
conveys the information.

**Totemic Attunement (14th Level)** — while raging, tied to your totem animal:
- **Bear**: hostile creatures within 5 ft. of you have disadvantage on attacks against targets other
  than you.
- **Eagle**: flying speed equal to your walking speed (you fall if you end your turn airborne).
- **Elk**: can move through a Large or smaller creature's space; that creature makes a STR save (DC
  8 + STR mod + proficiency) or is knocked prone and takes 1d12 + STR mod bludgeoning damage.
- **Tiger**: after moving 20+ ft. in a straight line toward a creature, make one additional melee
  attack against it.
- **Wolf**: can knock a Large or smaller creature prone when you hit it with a melee weapon attack.
