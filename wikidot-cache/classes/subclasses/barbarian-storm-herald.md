# Path of the Storm Herald (Barbarian)

Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Class Features

**Storm Aura (3rd Level)**
You emanate a stormy magical aura while raging, extending 10 feet from you (not through total
cover). Activates when you enter rage; reactivate as a bonus action each turn. Choose one
environment (fixed at 3rd level): DC = 8 + proficiency + CON mod.
- **Desert**: all other creatures in the aura take 2 fire damage (3 at 5th, 4 at 10th, 5 at 15th, 6
  at 20th level).
- **Sea**: one creature you choose in the aura makes a save, taking 1d6 lightning on a fail, half on
  a success (2d6 at 10th, 3d6 at 15th, 4d6 at 20th level).
- **Tundra**: each creature you choose in the aura gains 2 temporary HP (3 at 5th, 4 at 10th, 5 at
  15th, 6 at 20th level).

**Storm Soul (6th Level)**
Permanent benefit based on your environment:
- **Desert**: resistance to fire damage; immune to extreme heat; can ignite flammable objects as an
  action.
- **Sea**: resistance to lightning damage; can breathe underwater; gain a 30 ft. swimming speed.
- **Tundra**: resistance to cold damage; immune to extreme cold; can freeze a 5-ft. cube of water as
  an action (melts after 1 minute).

**Shielding Storm (10th Level)**
Each creature of your choice has the damage resistance you gained from Storm Soul while it is in
your Storm Aura.

**Raging Storm (14th Level)**
Environment-based reaction:
- **Desert**: after a creature hits you, it makes a DEX save or takes fire damage equal to half your
  barbarian level.
- **Sea**: when you hit a creature, it makes a STR save or is knocked prone.
- **Tundra**: when Storm Aura activates, one creature you choose makes a STR save or its speed is
  reduced to 0 until your next turn.
