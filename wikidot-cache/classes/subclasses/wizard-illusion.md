# School of Illusion (Wizard)

Fetched: 2026-09-05

## Class Features

**Illusion Savant (2nd Level)**
The gold and time you must spend to copy an illusion spell into your spellbook is halved.

**Improved Minor Illusion (2nd Level)**
You learn the Minor Illusion cantrip (or another wizard cantrip if you already know it); it doesn't
count against your number of cantrips known. When you cast it, you can create both a sound and an
image with a single casting.

**Malleable Illusions (6th Level)**
When you cast an illusion spell that has a duration of 1 minute or longer, you can use your action
to change the nature of that illusion (within the spell's normal parameters), as long as you can
see it.

**Illusory Self (10th Level)**
Reaction, when a creature you can see attacks you: interpose an illusory duplicate of yourself in
the attack's path; the attack automatically misses you, and the illusion then disappears. You can
use this feature once per short or long rest.

**Illusory Reality (14th Level)**
When you cast an illusion spell of 1st level or higher, you can use a bonus action to make one
inanimate, nonmagical object that is part of the illusion real for 1 minute; it can't deal damage or
otherwise directly harm anyone.
