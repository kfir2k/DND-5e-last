# Monk: Way of the Long Death

## Extracted content used

**Touch of Death (3rd level)**
When you reduce a creature within 5 feet of you to 0 hit points, gain temporary hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier + your monk level (minimum of 1).

**Hour of Reaping (6th level)**
As an action, each creature within 30 feet of you that can see you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

**Mastery of Death (11th level)**
When you are reduced to 0 hit points, you can expend 1 ki point (no action required) to have 1 hit point instead.

**Touch of the Long Death (17th level)**
Touch a creature and expend 1 to 10 ki points; the target makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 2d10 necrotic damage per ki point spent on a failure, or half as much on a success.
