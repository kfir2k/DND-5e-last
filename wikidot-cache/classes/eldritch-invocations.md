# Eldritch Invocations (Warlock)

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/warlock:eldritch-invocations
Fetched: 2026-09-22

Invocations Known by level lives on the main `warlock.md` level table (2/2/2/3/3/4/4/5/5/5/6/6/6/7/7/7/8/8/8
for levels 2-20). This page lists 72 total invocation entries; 54 are core/official (PHB/XGE/TCE — no
sourcebook or UA marker on the page), and the remaining 18 are explicitly UA/playtest material — see the
"Excluded (UA)" section at the bottom, out of scope per CLAUDE.md's UA policy.

Prerequisite column is verbatim from the page. Effect column is a concise mechanical paraphrase (numbers
verified against the page, not copied verbatim word-for-word — matches this repo's existing FEATURE_LIB
convention of short paraphrased descriptions).

list.
