# Kenku — dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:kenku

Cached 2026-09-22. Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:kenku

The page presents two versions: the current **Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse
(MotM)** version (no subraces), and the older **Volo's Guide to Monsters (VGtM)** version. Per this
app's existing convention (see `aasimar.md`), the MotM version is the one to use for new/edited
entries. No content on this page was marked (UA).

## Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse (current version — use this one)

**Ability Score Increase**
"increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different scores
by 1"

**Creature Type**
"You are a Humanoid."

**Size**
"Your size is Medium or Small. You choose the size when you select this race."

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 30 feet."

**Darkvision**
None — confirmed no darkvision trait on this version.

**Kenku Recall**
"Thanks to your supernaturally good memory, you have proficiency in two skills of your choice.
Moreover, when you make an ability check using any skill in which you have proficiency, you can
give yourself advantage on the check before rolling the d20. You can give yourself advantage in
this way a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when
you finish a long rest." — grants proficiency in 2 skills of choice; plus a limited-use
self-granted advantage (uses = proficiency bonus, recharges on long rest) on any check using a
skill you're proficient in.

**Expert Duplication**
"When you copy writing or craftwork produced by yourself or someone else, you have advantage on
any ability checks you make to produce an exact duplicate."

**Mimicry**
"You can accurately mimic sounds you have heard, including voices. A creature that hears the
sounds you make can tell they are imitations only with a successful Wisdom (Insight) check against
a DC of 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier." — DC = 8 + proficiency bonus +
Charisma modifier.

**Languages**
"Your character can speak, read, and write Common and one other language that you and your DM
agree is appropriate for the character." (Note: this differs from the older VGtM version below,
where Kenku could ONLY speak via Mimicry and had no normal Common/other-language speech.)

## Volo's Guide to Monsters (older version, reference only — not used by this app)

**Ability Score Increase**
"Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Wisdom score increases by 1."

**Age**
"They reach maturity at about 12 years old and can live to 60."

**Size**
"Your size is Medium."

**Speed**
"Your base walking speed is 30 feet."

**Darkvision**
None mentioned for this version either.

**Expert Forgery**
"You have advantage on all checks made to produce forgeries or duplicates"

**Kenku Training**
Skill proficiencies: "Acrobatics, Deception, Stealth, and Sleight of Hand" (fixed list, not a
choice, unlike MotM's "Kenku Recall").

**Mimicry**
"opposed by your Charisma (Deception) check" (rather than a flat DC 8 + proficiency + Charisma
formula — a listener's opposed Wisdom (Insight) check against the kenku's Deception check).

**Languages**
"you can speak only by using your Mimicry trait" — no normal spoken Common; could only communicate
by mimicking sounds/voices it had heard.
