# Goliath — dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:goliath

Cached 2026-09-22. Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:goliath

The page presents two versions of the race: the current **Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the
Multiverse (MotM)** version (no subraces), and the older **Volo's Guide to Monsters / Elemental Evil
Player's Companion (EEPC)** version. Both are recorded below for reference; per this app's existing
convention (see `aasimar.md`), the MotM version is the one to use for new/edited entries.

## Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse (current version — use this one)

**Ability Score Increase**
"increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different scores
by 1"

**Size**
Medium. Goliaths stand 7-8 feet tall.

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 30 feet."

**Darkvision**
None — no darkvision trait present on this version.

**Little Giant**
"proficiency in the Athletics skill, and you count as one size larger when determining your
carrying capacity" — this trait folds together what the older version split into separate Natural
Athlete and Powerful Build traits.

**Mountain Born**
"resistance to cold damage. You also naturally acclimate to high altitudes" — i.e. resistance to
cold damage, plus acclimatization to high altitude (no game-mechanical penalty from thin air/cold
climates).

**Stone's Endurance**
"roll a d12. Add your Constitution modifier to the number rolled and reduce the damage by that
total. You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain
all expended uses when you finish a long rest." (Confirmed via follow-up verbatim fetch
2026-09-22 — uses = proficiency bonus, recharge on long rest.)

**Languages**
"Common and one other language that you and your DM agree is appropriate"

## Elemental Evil Player's Companion / Volo's Guide (older version, reference only — not used by this app)

**Ability Score Increase**
"Strength score increases by 2, and your Constitution score increases by 1"

**Size**
Medium.

**Speed**
30 feet walking speed.

**Natural Athlete**
"proficiency in the Athletics skill"

**Stone's Endurance**
"roll a d12...reduce the damage by that total. After you use this trait, you can't use it again
until you finish a short or long rest" — i.e. once per short or long rest (not proficiency-bonus
uses, unlike the MotM version above).

**Powerful Build**
"count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity"

**Mountain Born**
"resistance to cold damage" (this older version does not mention high-altitude acclimatization).

**Languages**
"Common and Giant"
