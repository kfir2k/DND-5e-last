# Satyr — dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:satyr

Cached 2026-09-22. Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:satyr

The page presents two versions: the current **Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse
(MotM)** version (no subraces), and the older **Mythic Odysseys of Theros (MOoT)** version. Per
this app's existing convention (see `aasimar.md`), the MotM version is the one to use for new/edited
entries. No content on this page was marked (UA).

## Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse (current version — use this one)

**Ability Score Increase**
"increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different scores
by 1"

**Creature Type**
"You are a Fey."

**Size**
"You are Medium."

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 35 feet."

**Darkvision**
None — no darkvision trait present on this version.

**Ram**
"deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage" — this is the unarmed-strike-style butt
attack; 1d6 + Str mod bludgeoning.

**Magic Resistance**
"You have advantage on saving throws against spells."

**Mirthful Leaps**
"roll a d8 and add the number rolled to the number of feet you cover" — when high/long jumping,
add 1d8 feet to the distance covered (on top of normal jump distance).

**Reveler**
"proficiency in the Performance and Persuasion skills, and you have proficiency with one musical
instrument"

**Languages**
"Common and one other language that you and your DM agree is appropriate"

## Mythic Odysseys of Theros (older version, reference only — not used by this app)

**Ability Score Increase**
"Your Charisma score increases by 2, and your Dexterity score increases by 1."

**Size**
"Your size is medium."

**Speed**
"Your base walking speed is 35 feet."

**Fey**
"Your creature type is fey, rather than humanoid."

**Ram**
"deal bludgeoning damage equal to 1d4 + your Strength modifier" — note this older version's Ram
die is 1d4, smaller than the MotM version's 1d6.

**Magic Resistance**
"advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects" (slightly broader wording
than MotM's "against spells" alone — explicitly includes "other magical effects").

**Mirthful Leaps**
"roll a d8 and add the number to the number of feet you cover" (same d8 as MotM version).

**Reveler**
"proficiency in the Performance and Persuasion skills, and proficiency with one musical
instrument" (same as MotM version).

**Languages**
"Common and Sylvan." (fixed language list, unlike MotM's "one other language... DM agrees").
