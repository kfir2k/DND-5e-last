# Fairy — dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:fairy

Cached 2026-09-22. Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:fairy

The page presents the current official version, which appears essentially identically worded in
both **Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse (MotM)** and **The Wild Beyond the
Witchlight (WBtW)** (WBtW adds a non-mechanical flavor note that fairies "have a life span of
about a century"; no trait-text differences found between the two). The page also has a separate
**Unearthed Arcana (UA)** version with different/additional traits — excluded from this report
per CLAUDE.md scope (UA), noted only so it's known to exist on the page.

## Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse / Wild Beyond the Witchlight (current, official)

Trait heading order on the page: Ability Score Increase, Creature Type, Size, Speed, Fairy
Magic, Flight, Languages.

**Ability Score Increase**
"Increase one ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1, or increase three
different scores by 1."

**Creature Type**
"You are a Fey."

**Size**
"You are Small."

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 30 feet."

**Fairy Magic**
"You know the Druidcraft cantrip. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast the Faerie Fire spell with
this trait. Starting at 5th level, you can also cast the Enlarge/Reduce spell with this trait.
Once you cast Faerie Fire or Enlarge/Reduce with this trait, you can't cast that spell with it
again until you finish a long rest."

**Flight**
"You have a flying speed equal to your walking speed. You can't use this flying speed if you're
wearing medium or heavy armor."

**Languages**
"You can speak, read, and write Common and one other language that you and your DM agree is
appropriate for the character."

No darkvision listed for this race. No subraces.

## Unearthed Arcana version (excluded — not official)

Page also lists a UA variant with a differently-worded Ability Score Increase ("Increase one
ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1" — fixed, no three-way option), a "Fairy
Flight" trait that adds hovering, Fairy Magic without the long-rest limit on Faerie Fire, and an
additional **Fey Passage** trait ("You can squeeze through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide") —
not verified/quoted in full here since UA content is out of scope per CLAUDE.md.
