# Shadar-kai — dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:shadar-kai

Cached 2026-09-22. Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:shadar-kai

The page presents two versions: the current **Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse
(MotM)** unified Shadar-kai (no subraces — this is the version this app should use, matching the
`motm:true` pattern), and the older **Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes (MToF)** version. Both recorded
below for reference.

## Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse (current version this app should use)

**Ability Score Increase**
"When determining your character's ability scores, increase one score by 2 and increase a
different score by 1, or increase three different scores by 1."

**Creature Type**
"You are a Humanoid. You are also considered an elf for any prerequisite or effect that requires
you to be an elf."

**Size**
"You are Medium."

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 30 feet."

**Darkvision**
"You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if
it were dim light."

**Fey Ancestry**
"You have advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the charmed condition on yourself."

**Trance**
"You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep. You can finish a long rest in 4 hours
if you spend those hours in a trancelike meditation, during which you retain consciousness.
Whenever you finish this trance, you can gain two proficiencies that you don't have, each one with
a weapon or a tool of your choice selected from the Player's Handbook. You mystically acquire
these proficiencies by drawing them from shared elven memory, and you retain them until you finish
your next long rest."

**Keen Senses**
"You have proficiency in the Perception skill."

**Necrotic Resistance**
"You have resistance to necrotic damage."

**Blessing of the Raven Queen**
"As a bonus action, you can magically teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see.
You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all
expended uses when you finish a long rest. Starting at 3rd level, you also gain resistance to all
damage when you teleport using this trait. The resistance lasts until the start of your next turn.
During that time, you appear ghostly and translucent."

**Languages**
"Your character can speak, read, and write Common and one other language that you and your DM
agree is appropriate."

## Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes (older version, kept for reference only — not used by this app)

**Ability Score Increase**: "Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Constitution score
increases by 1."
**Size**: "Your size is Medium."
**Speed**: "Your base walking speed is 30 feet."
**Darkvision**: "You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in
darkness as if it were dim light."
**Fey Ancestry**: "You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put
you to sleep."
**Necrotic Resistance**: "You have resistance to necrotic damage."
**Blessing of the Raven Queen**: "As a bonus action, you can magically teleport up to 30 feet to
an unoccupied space you can see. Once you use this trait, you can't do so again until you finish
a long rest." Starting at 3rd level, gains damage resistance until the start of the next turn when
teleporting this way, appearing ghostly and translucent (same flavor as MotM, but MotM upgrades
uses-per-long-rest from a flat 1 to proficiency-bonus-many, and ties the resistance upgrade to the
same 3rd-level gate).
**Languages**: "You can speak, read, and write Common and Elven."
