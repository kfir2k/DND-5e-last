# Tiefling

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/tiefling
Fetched: 2026-09-22

## Core racial traits (Player's Handbook)

- **Ability Score Increase:** Charisma +2 (some printings also give Intelligence +1; the page lists CHA +2
  as the baseline PHB trait used here)
- **Age:** Matures at the same rate as humans; lives a few years longer than humans
- **Alignment:** No innate leaning toward evil despite infernal heritage, but many end up there anyway;
  independent nature often produces a chaotic bent
- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Darkvision:** See in dim light within 60 ft. as if bright light, and in darkness as if dim light; can't
  discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
- **Hellish Resistance:** Resistance to fire damage.
- **Infernal Legacy:** Know the *thaumaturgy* cantrip. At 3rd level, cast *hellish rebuke* once as a
  2nd-level spell (no slot needed); at 5th level, cast *darkness* once — both recharge on a long rest.
  Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these.
- **Languages:** Common and Infernal.

## Official subrace/variant options (out of scope for this repo unless noted)

- **Nine infernal-bloodline variants** (Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes) — Asmodeus, Baalzebul, Dispater,
  Fierna, Glasya, Levistus, Mammon, Mephistopheles, Zariel — each swaps the ability bonuses and Infernal
  Legacy's granted spells for a bloodline-specific set. Not yet added to this repo's RACES/RACE_LIB; would
  need their own dedicated verbatim-quoted pass per bloodline before adding.
- **Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide variants:** Feral, Devil's Tongue, Hellfire, Winged.
- **Unearthed Arcana:** Abyssal Tiefling (random daily spell) — UA, excluded per this repo's policy.

This fetch's scope was limited to verifying the base PHB trait split (Infernal Legacy's 3rd/5th-level
spell unlocks) for the level-up-picker overhaul — the bloodline variants above are noted for a future pass,
not verified/added here.
