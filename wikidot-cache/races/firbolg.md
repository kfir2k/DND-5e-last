Cached 2026-09-22. Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:firbolg

The page presents two versions: the current **Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the
Multiverse (MotM)** version (no subraces) and the older **Volo's Guide to Monsters (VGtM)**
version. No content on this page is marked (UA). Both are recorded below for reference; prefer
the MotM version for new/edited entries in `data-libraries.js` unless the app already commits to
VGtM elsewhere.

## Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse (current version)

Trait order on page: Ability Score Increase, Creature Type, Size, Speed, Firbolg Magic, Hidden
Step, Powerful Build, Speech of Beast and Leaf, Languages. **No Darkvision trait in this
version.**

**Ability Score Increase**
"Increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different
scores by 1."

**Creature Type**
"You are a Humanoid."

**Size**
"You are Medium."

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 30 feet."

**Firbolg Magic**
"You can cast the Detect Magic and Disguise Self spells with this trait." (Disguise Self note
from summary: can appear 3 feet shorter or taller than actual.) "Once you cast either of these
spells with this trait, you can't cast that spell with it again until you finish a long rest."
"Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells when you cast
them with this trait."

**Hidden Step**
"As a bonus action, you can magically turn invisible until the start of your next turn or until
you attack, make a damage roll, or force someone to make a saving throw." "You can use this
trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when
you finish a long rest."

**Powerful Build**
"You count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can
push, drag, or lift."

**Speech of Beast and Leaf**
"You have the ability to communicate in a limited manner with Beasts, Plants, and vegetation."
"You have advantage on all Charisma checks you make to influence them."

**Languages**
"Your character can speak, read, and write Common and one other language that you and your DM
agree is appropriate for the character."

## Volo's Guide to Monsters (older version, kept for reference only)

**Ability Score Increase**: Wisdom +2, Strength +1.
**Age/Lifespan**: reach adulthood around 30; can live to 500 years.
**Alignment**: "typically neutral good."
**Size**: 7–8 feet tall; Medium.
**Speed**: 30 feet.
**Firbolg Magic**: Detect Magic and Disguise Self, using Wisdom as spellcasting ability; usable
once per short or long rest (rather than MotM's "once per long rest, per spell").
**Hidden Step**: bonus-action invisibility; usable once per short or long rest (MotM instead
scales uses to proficiency bonus per long rest).
**Powerful Build**: same as MotM version.
**Speech of Beast and Leaf**: same core ability as MotM version.
**Languages**: Common, Elvish, and Giant (MotM instead grants Common + one language of choice).
