**Flavor (three intro paragraphs, no separate Age/Alignment section)**
"Hadozees' progenitors were mammals no bigger than housecats." Paraphrase: hunted by larger
predators, they took to the trees and evolved wing-like flaps for gliding between branches.
"Today, hadozees are sapient, bipedal beings eager to leave behind the fearsome predators of their
homeworld and explore other worlds." Paraphrase: natural climbers whose feet are as dexterous as
their hands (opposable thumbs); skin membranes hang from arms and legs and let them glide when
stretched taut; they wrap the membranes around themselves to keep warm.

## Hadozee Traits

**Ability Score Increase**

**Creature Type**

**Size**

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 30 feet, and you have a climbing speed equal to your walking speed."

**Darkvision**
None — no darkvision trait present.

**Dexterous Feet**
"As a bonus action, you can use your feet to manipulate an object, open or close a door or
container," — paraphrase of the rest: or pick up or set down a Tiny object.

**Glide**
"When you fall at least 10 feet above the ground, you can use your reaction to extend your skin
membranes" — paraphrase of the rest: to glide horizontally a number of feet equal to your walking
speed, and you take 0 damage from the fall; you determine the direction of the glide.

**Hadozee Dodge**
"The magic that runs in your veins heightens your natural defenses."
"When you take damage, you can use your reaction to roll a d6." — paraphrase: add your proficiency
bonus to the number rolled and reduce the damage you take by that total (minimum of 0 damage).
"You can use this trait a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus."
"You regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest."

**Languages**
"You can speak, read, and write Common and one other language that you and your DM agree is
appropriate."
