# Genasi (Air) — dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:genasi-air

Cached 2026-09-22. Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:genasi-air

**Note:** Genasi are NOT one shared wikidot page — Air/Earth/Fire/Water each have their own
separate `lineage:genasi-<element>` page with fully distinct trait text (confirmed by fetching
each page directly, not just the `/lineage` index). See also `genasi-earth.md`,
`genasi-fire.md`, `genasi-water.md`. No content on this page is marked (UA).

The page presents two versions: the current **Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the
Multiverse (MotM)** version and the older **Elemental Evil Player's Companion (EEPC)** version.

## Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse (current version)

Trait order: Ability Score Increase, Creature Type, Size, Speed, Darkvision, Unending Breath,
Mingle with the Wind, Languages.

**Ability Score Increase**
"Increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different
scores by 1."

**Creature Type**
"You are a Humanoid."

**Size**
"You are Medium or Small. You choose the size when you select this race."

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 35 feet."

**Darkvision**
"You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light and in darkness as
if it were dim light."

**Unending Breath**
"You can hold your breath indefinitely while you're not incapacitated."

**Mingle with the Wind**
"You know the Shocking Grasp cantrip. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast Feather Fall. Starting
at 5th level, you can also cast Levitate. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting
ability." (Pattern matches other MotM genasi: each leveled spell usable once per long rest,
also castable with spell slots of appropriate level once known.)

**Languages**
"Your character can speak, read, and write Common and one other language that you and your DM
agree is appropriate for the character."

## Elemental Evil Player's Companion (older version, kept for reference only)

**Ability Score Increase**: "Your Constitution score increases by 2, and Dexterity score
increases by 1."
**Speed**: "Your base walking speed is 30 feet."
**Darkvision**: not listed in this version.
**Unending Breath**: "You can hold your breath indefinitely while you're not incapacitated."
(identical to MotM wording)
**Mingle with the Wind**: "You can cast the Levitate spell once with this trait, requiring no
material components. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for this spell." Regains after a
long rest. (EEPC grants only Levitate — no cantrip, no Feather Fall — unlike MotM's three-spell
progression.)
**Languages**: Common and Primordial.
