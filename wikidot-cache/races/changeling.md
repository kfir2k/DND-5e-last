# Changeling — dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:changeling

Cached 2026-09-22. Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:changeling

The page presents three versions: the current **Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the
Multiverse (MotM)** version, the older **Eberron: Rising from the Last War** version (fixed
Cha +2 / +1 choice), and an **Unearthed Arcana (UA)** version with additional traits
(Unsettling Visage, Divergent Persona) — the UA traits are excluded from app scope per
CLAUDE.md; noted here only so a future session knows they exist on the page and are not
official.

## Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse (current)

Trait heading order on the page: Ability Score Increase, Creature Type, Size, Speed, Changeling
Instincts, Shapechanger, Languages.

**Ability Score Increase**
"Increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different
scores by 1."

**Creature Type**
"You are a Fey."

**Size**
"You are Medium or Small. You choose the size when you select this race."

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 30 feet."

**Changeling Instincts**
"You gain proficiency with two of the following skills of your choice: Deception, Insight,
Intimidation, Performance, or Persuasion."

**Shapechanger**
"As an action, you can change your appearance and your voice. You stay in the new form until
you use an action to revert to your true form or until you die."

**Languages**
"Your character can speak, read, and write Common and one other language that you and your DM
agree is appropriate."

No darkvision listed for this version.

## Eberron: Rising from the Last War (older version, kept for reference)

**Ability Score Increase**: "Your Charisma score increases by 2. In addition, one other ability
score of your choice increases by 1."
**Size**: "Your size is Medium."
**Speed**: "Your base walking speed is 30 feet."
**Shapechanger**: "As an action, you can change your appearance and your voice. You determine
the specifics of the changes, including your coloration, hair length, and sex."
**Changeling Instincts**: "You gain proficiency with two of the following skills of your choice:
Deception, Insight, Intimidation, and Persuasion." (note: Performance is NOT in this older
version's list, unlike the MotM version)
**Languages**: "You can speak, read, and write Common and two other languages of your choice."

## Unearthed Arcana version (excluded — not official)

Page also lists a UA variant with **Unsettling Visage** and **Divergent Persona** traits — not
verified/quoted here since UA content is out of scope per CLAUDE.md.

No subraces for any official version.
