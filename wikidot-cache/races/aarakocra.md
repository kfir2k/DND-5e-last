# Aarakocra — dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:aarakocra

Cached 2026-09-22. Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:aarakocra

The page presents two versions: the current **Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse
(MotM)** version (flexible ASI, no fixed ability scores) and the older **Elemental Evil Player's
Companion (EEPC)** version (fixed Dex +2/Wis +1). Neither version is marked (UA). Both are
recorded below for reference; MotM is the current official version.

## Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse (current)

Trait heading order on the page: Ability Score Increase, Creature Type, Size, Speed, Flight,
Talons, Wind Caller, Languages.

**Ability Score Increase**
"increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different
scores by 1"

**Creature Type**
"You are a Humanoid."

**Size**
"Your size is Medium."

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 30 feet."

**Flight**
"you have a flying speed equal to your walking speed. You can't use this flying speed if
you're wearing medium or heavy armor."

**Talons**
"You have talons that you can use to make unarmed strikes. When you hit with them, the strike
deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier slashing damage, instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for
an unarmed strike."

**Wind Caller**
"Starting at 3rd level, you can cast the Gust of Wind spell with this trait, without requiring a
material component. Once you cast the spell with this trait, you can't do so again until you
finish a long rest." Spellcasting ability is Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (your choice, made
when you select this race).

**Languages**
"speak, read, and write Common and one other language" that you and your DM agree is appropriate.

## Elemental Evil Player's Companion (older version, kept for reference)

**Ability Score Increase**: "Your Dexterity score increases by 2, and your Wisdom score
increases by 1."
**Size**: "Your size is Medium."
**Speed**: "Your base walking speed is 25 feet."
**Flight**: "You have a flying speed of 50 feet. To use this speed, you can't be wearing medium
or heavy armor."
**Talons**: unarmed strike, "deal 1d4 slashing damage on a hit" instead of bludgeoning.
**Languages**: "speak, read, and write Common, Aarakocra, and Auran."

No subraces for either version.
