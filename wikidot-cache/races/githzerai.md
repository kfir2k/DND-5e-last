# Githzerai — dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:githzerai

Cached 2026-09-22. Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:githzerai

The page presents two versions: the current **Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the
Multiverse (MotM)** version and the older **Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes (MToF)** version. No
content on this page is marked (UA). Neither version has a Darkvision trait — Gith races are
notably one of the few humanoid lineages without darkvision.

## Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse (current version)

Trait order: Ability Score Increase, Creature Type, Size, Speed, Githzerai Psionics, Mental
Discipline, Psychic Resilience, Languages. **No Darkvision.**

**Ability Score Increase**
"Increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different
scores by 1."

**Creature Type**
"You are a Humanoid."

**Size**
"You are Medium."

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 30 feet."

**Githzerai Psionics**
"You know the Mage Hand cantrip, and the hand is invisible when you cast the cantrip with this
trait. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast the Shield spell with this trait. Starting at 5th
level, you can also cast the Detect Thoughts spell with this trait." No material components
required. "Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability" for these (chosen
when the race is selected). Consistent with other MotM lineages: each leveled spell usable once
per long rest via the trait, also castable with spell slots of the appropriate level once known.

**Mental Discipline**
"Your innate psychic defenses grant you advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the
charmed and frightened conditions on yourself."

**Psychic Resilience**
"You have resistance to psychic damage."

**Languages**
"Your character can speak, read, and write Common and one other language that you and your DM
agree is appropriate for the character."

## Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes (older version, kept for reference only)

Trait order: Ability Score Increase, Age, Size, Speed, Alignment, Mental Discipline, Githzerai
Psionics, Languages, Gith Tables.

**Ability Score Increase**: Wisdom +2, Intelligence +1.
**Age**: "Gith reach adulthood in their late teens and live for about a century."
**Size**: Medium (about 6 feet tall).
**Speed**: 30 feet.
**Alignment**: "Githzerai tend toward lawful neutral. Their rigorous training in psychic
abilities requires an implacable mental discipline."
**Mental Discipline**: "advantage on saving throws against the charmed and frightened
conditions" — same effect as MotM's version, near-identical wording.
**Githzerai Psionics**: same Mage Hand/Shield(3rd)/Detect Thoughts(5th) progression as MotM, but
Wisdom-only spellcasting ability rather than a choice of Int/Wis/Cha; no material components,
same as MotM.
**Languages**: "You can speak, read, and write Common and Gith."
**Note**: MToF version has no separate Psychic Resilience trait (that resistance-to-psychic-
damage trait is new to the MotM version) and no Creature Type trait listed separately.
