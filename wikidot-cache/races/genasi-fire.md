# Genasi (Fire) — dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:genasi-fire

Cached 2026-09-22. Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:genasi-fire

**Note:** Genasi are NOT one shared wikidot page — each element has its own separate
`lineage:genasi-<element>` page with distinct trait text. See also `genasi-air.md`,
`genasi-earth.md`, `genasi-water.md`. No content on this page is marked (UA).

The page presents two versions: the current **Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the
Multiverse (MotM)** version and the older **Elemental Evil Player's Companion (EEPC)** version.

## Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse (current version)

Trait order: Ability Score Increase, Creature Type, Size, Speed, Darkvision, Fire Resistance,
Reach to the Blaze, Languages.

**Ability Score Increase**
"Increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different
scores by 1."

**Creature Type**
"You are a Humanoid."

**Size**
"You are Medium or Small (player's choice)."

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 30 feet."

**Darkvision**
"You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light and in darkness as
if it were dim light."

**Fire Resistance**
Resistance to fire damage.

**Reach to the Blaze**
"You know the Produce Flame cantrip. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast the Burning Hands spell
with this trait. Starting at 5th level, you can also cast the Flame Blade spell with this trait,
without requiring a material component." "Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting
ability for these spells when you cast them with this trait (choose when you select this race)."
(Consistent with other MotM genasi: each leveled spell usable once per long rest, also castable
with spell slots of appropriate level once known.)

**Languages**
"Your character can speak, read, and write Common and one other language that you and your DM
agree is appropriate for the character."

## Elemental Evil Player's Companion (older version, kept for reference only)

**Ability Score Increase**: Constitution +2, Intelligence +1.
**Age**: matures like humans, reaches adulthood in late teens; lives up to 120 years.
**Alignment**: tends toward neutral.
**Size**: Medium.
**Speed**: 30 feet.
**Darkvision**: "see in dim light within 60 feet as if bright light; in darkness as if dim
light; everything appears in shades of red" (same 60-ft range as MotM, flavor differs).
**Fire Resistance**: resistance to fire damage (same as MotM).
**Reach to the Blaze**: know Produce Flame; cast Burning Hands once, regain after long rest (no
3rd/5th-level gating and no Flame Blade — EEPC's version is flatter than MotM's leveled
progression).
**Languages**: Common and Primordial.
