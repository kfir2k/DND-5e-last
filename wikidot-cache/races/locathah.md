# Locathah — dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:locathah

Cached 2026-09-22. Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:locathah

Source: Locathah Rising (Extra Life 2019). No subraces. No content on this page was marked (UA).
This is a single-version race page (no separate MotM revision, unlike Aasimar/Goliath/Kenku/Satyr).

**Ability Score Increase**
"Your Strength score increases by 2 and your Dexterity score increases by 1." — confirms the
`bonus:{str:2,dex:1}` already in `data-classes-races.js` for Locathah is correct.

**Age**
"Locathah mature to adulthood by the age of 10 but have been known to live up to 80 years."

**Alignment**
"Most locathah are true neutral or have some aspect of neutrality in their alignment. They tend
toward good, coming from a culture where compassion for the downtrodden is a commonality."

**Size**
"Locathah stand between 5 and 6 feet tall and average about 150 pounds. Your size is Medium."

**Speed**
"Your base walking speed is 30 feet, and you have a swim speed of 30 feet." — 30 ft. walk, 30 ft.
swim.

**Darkvision**
None — no darkvision trait listed on this page.

**Natural Armor**
"You have tough, scaly skin. When you aren't wearing armor, your AC is 12 + your Dexterity
modifier. You can use your natural armor to determine your AC if the armor you wear would leave
you with a lower AC. A shield's benefits apply as normal while you use your natural armor." —
unarmored AC = 12 + Dex mod; usable in place of worn armor if that would be worse; shields still
stack normally.

**Observant and Athletic**
"You have proficiency in the Athletics and Perception skills."

**Leviathan Will**
"You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned,
stunned, or put to sleep." — advantage on saves vs. 6 named conditions: charmed, frightened,
paralyzed, poisoned, stunned, put to sleep (the "sleep" condition specifically, e.g. the *sleep*
spell).

**Limited Amphibiousness**
"You can breathe air and water, but you need to be submerged at least once every 4 hours to avoid
suffocating." — must be submerged at least once per 4 hours or begins to suffocate.

**Languages**
"You can speak, read, and write Aquan and Common."
