# Githyanki — dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:githyanki

Cached 2026-09-22. Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/lineage:githyanki

The page presents two versions: the current **Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the
Multiverse (MotM)** version and the older **Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes (MToF)** version. No
content on this page is marked (UA). Neither version has a Darkvision trait — Gith races are
notably one of the few humanoid lineages without darkvision.

## Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse (current version)

Trait order: Ability Score Increase, Creature Type, Size, Speed, Astral Knowledge, Githyanki
Psionics, Psychic Resilience, Languages. **No Darkvision. No Martial Prodigy/weapon-proficiency
trait in this version** (that proficiency grant moved into MToF's separate trait, dropped here).

**Ability Score Increase**
"Increase one score by 2 and increase a different score by 1, or increase three different
scores by 1."

**Creature Type**
"You are a Humanoid."

**Size**
"You are Medium."

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 30 feet."

**Astral Knowledge**
"You can mystically access a reservoir of experiences of entities connected to the Astral
Plane" — grants proficiency in one skill of your choice and with one weapon or tool of your
choice; "These proficiencies last until the end of your next long rest" (i.e. re-rolled/re-chosen
each long rest, not permanent).

**Githyanki Psionics**
"You know the Mage Hand cantrip, and the hand is invisible when you cast the cantrip with this
trait. Starting at 3rd level, you can cast the Jump spell with this trait. Starting at 5th
level, you can also cast the Misty Step spell with this trait. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma
is your spellcasting ability" for these. (Consistent with other MotM lineages: each leveled spell
usable once per long rest via the trait, also castable with spell slots of the appropriate level
once known.)

**Psychic Resilience**
"You have resistance to psychic damage."

**Languages**
"Your character can speak, read, and write Common and one other language that you and your DM
agree is appropriate for the character."

## Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes (older version, kept for reference only)

Trait order: Ability Score Increase, Age, Size, Speed, Alignment, Decadent Mastery, Martial
Prodigy, Githyanki Psionics, Languages.

**Ability Score Increase**: "Your Strength score increases by 2, and your Intelligence score
increases by 1."
**Age**: "Gith reach adulthood in their late teens and live for about a century."
**Size**: "Your size is Medium."
**Speed**: "Your base walking speed is 30 feet."
**Alignment**: "Githyanki tend toward lawful evil. They are aggressive and arrogant..."
**Decadent Mastery**: "You learn one language of your choice, and you are proficient with one
skill or tool of your choice." (unlike MotM's Astral Knowledge, this is a permanent,
once-and-done grant, not refreshed per long rest.)
**Martial Prodigy**: "You are proficient with light and medium armor and with shortswords,
longswords, and greatswords." (this weapon/armor proficiency trait does not carry over into the
MotM version at all.)
**Githyanki Psionics**: same three-spell progression as MotM (Mage Hand invisible cantrip,
Jump at 3rd, Misty Step at 5th) but Intelligence-only spellcasting ability rather than a choice
of Int/Wis/Cha.
**Languages**: "You can speak, read, and write Common and Gith."
