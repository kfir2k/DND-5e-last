Source: Strixhaven: A Curriculum of Chaos (SCC). No subraces. No content on this page was marked
(UA). Single-version race page.

**Ability Score Increase**
"Increase one ability score by 2, and increase a different one by 1." (page did not separately
surface a "or three different scores by 1" alternate-array clause the way MotM-era races do — the
quote returned was specifically the 2/1 split; treat as the sole array unless a re-fetch turns up
an additional "or three by 1" sentence.)

**Creature Type**
"You are a Humanoid."

**Size**
"You are Medium or Small. You choose the size when you select this race."

**Speed**
"Your walking speed is 30 feet."

**Darkvision**
"You can see in dim light within 120 feet of yourself as if it were bright light and in darkness
as if it were dim light. You discern colors in that darkness only as shades of gray." — 120 ft.
darkvision, which confirms the existing `dark:120` already set for Owlin in
`data-classes-races.js`.

**Flight**
"Thanks to your wings, you have a flying speed equal to your walking speed. You can't use this
flying speed if you're wearing medium or heavy armor." — flying speed = walking speed (30 ft.);
disabled while wearing medium or heavy armor.

**Silent Feathers**
"You have proficiency in the Stealth skill."

**Languages**
"You can speak, read, and write Common and one other language that you and your DM agree is
appropriate for the character."
