# Elf

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/elf
Fetched: 2026-09-22

## Base elf traits (Player's Handbook)

- **Ability Score Increase:** Dexterity +2
- **Age:** Reaches adulthood around 100 years old; can live to 750 years.
- **Alignment:** Lean toward chaos and good, valuing freedom/self-expression (Drow, exiled to the
  Underdark, tend toward evil instead).
- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **Darkvision:** See in dim light within 60 ft. as if bright light, and in darkness as if dim light;
  can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.
- **Fey Ancestry:** Advantage on saves against being charmed; magic can't put you to sleep.
- **Trance:** Meditate deeply 4 hours a day instead of sleeping 8, and gain the same benefit a human gets
  from 8 hours of sleep.
- **Keen Senses:** Proficiency in Perception.
- **Languages:** Common and Elvish.

## Subraces

### High Elf
- **Ability Increase:** Intelligence +1
- **Cantrip:** One Wizard cantrip of your choice; Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for it.
- **Elf Weapon Training:** Proficiency with longsword, shortsword, shortbow, longbow.
- **Extra Language:** One additional language of your choice.

### Wood Elf
- **Ability Increase:** Wisdom +1
- **Elf Weapon Training:** Proficiency with longsword, shortsword, shortbow, longbow.
- **Fleet of Foot:** Base walking speed increases to 35 ft.
- **Mask of the Wild:** Can attempt to hide even when only lightly obscured by foliage, heavy rain,
  falling snow, mist, or other natural phenomena.

### Dark Elf (Drow)
- **Ability Increase:** Charisma +1
- **Superior Darkvision:** Darkvision range extends to 120 ft. (instead of the usual 60).
- **Sunlight Sensitivity:** Disadvantage on attack rolls and on Perception checks that rely on sight when
  you, the target, or whatever you're trying to perceive is in direct sunlight.
- **Drow Magic:** Know the *dancing lights* cantrip (CHA-based) at 1st level. At 3rd level, cast
  *faerie fire* once; at 5th level, cast *darkness* once — both 1/day, recharging on a long rest, CHA-based.
- **Drow Weapon Training:** Proficiency with rapiers, shortswords, hand crossbows.
