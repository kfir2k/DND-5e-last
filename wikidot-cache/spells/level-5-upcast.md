# 5th-Level Spells — "At Higher Levels" Upcast Notes

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:<slug> (individual spell pages), fetched 2026-09-22.
Numeric scaling facts only (verbatim/near-verbatim from each page's "At Higher Levels" section),
not full rules text.

**Animate Objects** — Animate two additional objects for each slot level above 5th.
**Antilife Shell** — No upcast benefit.
**Awaken** — No upcast benefit.
**Banishing Smite** — No upcast benefit.
**Bigby's Hand** — Damage from the clenched fist option increases by 2d8, and damage from the grasping hand option increases by 2d6, for each slot level above 5th.
**Circle of Power** — No upcast benefit.
**Cloudkill** — Damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 5th.
**Commune** — No upcast benefit.
**Commune with Nature** — No upcast benefit.
**Cone of Cold** — Damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 5th.
**Conjure Elemental** — Challenge rating of the elemental you can summon increases by 1 for each slot level above 5th.
**Conjure Volley** — No upcast benefit.
**Contact Other Plane** — No upcast benefit.
**Contagion** — No upcast benefit.
**Control Winds** — No upcast benefit.
**Creation** — Cube size increases by 5 feet (per side) for each slot level above 5th.
**Danse Macabre** — Animate up to two additional corpses/bones for each slot level above 5th.
**Dawn** — No upcast benefit.
**Destructive Wave** — No upcast benefit.
**Dispel Evil and Good** — No upcast benefit.
**Dominate Person** — Concentration duration: 6th-level slot = up to 10 minutes; 7th-level slot = up to 1 hour; 8th-level slot or higher = up to 8 hours.
**Dream** — No upcast benefit.
**Enervation** — Damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 5th.
**Far Step** — No upcast benefit.
**Flame Strike** — The fire damage or the radiant damage (your choice) increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 5th.
**Geas** — Duration: 7th- or 8th-level slot = 1 year; 9th-level slot = lasts until ended by remove curse/greater restoration/wish.
**Greater Restoration** — No upcast benefit.
**Hallow** — No upcast benefit.
**Hold Monster** — When cast with a slot of 6th level or higher, target one additional creature for each slot level above 5th.
**Holy Weapon** — No upcast benefit.
**Immolation** — No upcast benefit. (Confirmed via full verbatim text — no "At Higher Levels" paragraph exists on the page.)
**Infernal Calling** — Challenge rating of the devil you can summon increases by 1 for each slot level above 5th.
**Insect Plague** — Damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 5th.
**Legend Lore** — No upcast benefit.
**Maelstrom** — No upcast benefit. (Confirmed via full verbatim text — no "At Higher Levels" paragraph exists on the page.)
**Mass Cure Wounds** — Healing increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 5th.
**Mislead** — No upcast benefit.
**Modify Memory** — How far back you can reach into the target's memory: 6th-level slot = up to 7 days ago; 7th-level slot = up to 30 days ago; 8th-level slot = up to 1 year ago; 9th-level slot = any time in the creature's past.
**Negative Energy Flood** — No upcast benefit. (Confirmed via full verbatim text — no "At Higher Levels" paragraph exists on the page.)
**Passwall** — No upcast benefit. (Confirmed via full verbatim text — no "At Higher Levels" paragraph exists on the page.)
**Planar Binding** — Duration when cast with a higher-level slot: 6th-level slot = 10 days; 7th-level slot = 30 days; 8th-level slot = 180 days; 9th-level slot = 1 year and 1 day.
**Raise Dead** — No upcast benefit.
**Rary's Telepathic Bond** — No upcast benefit.
**Reincarnate** — No upcast benefit.
