# 4th-Level Spells — "At Higher Levels" Upcast Notes

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:<slug> (individual spell pages), fetched 2026-09-22.
Numeric scaling facts only (verbatim/near-verbatim from each page's "At Higher Levels" section),
not full rules text.

**Arcane Eye** — No upcast benefit.
**Aura of Life** — No upcast benefit.
**Aura of Purity** — No upcast benefit.
**Banishment** — When cast with a slot of 5th level or higher, target one additional creature for each slot level above 4th.
**Blight** — Damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 4th.
**Charm Monster** — When cast with a slot of 5th level or higher, target one additional creature for each slot level above 4th.
**Compulsion** — No upcast benefit.
**Confusion** — Radius increases by 5 feet for each slot level above 4th.
**Conjure Minor Elementals** — Summon twice as many creatures with a 6th-level slot, three times as many with an 8th-level slot.
**Conjure Woodland Beings** — Summon twice as many creatures with a 6th-level slot, three times as many with an 8th-level slot.
**Control Water** — No upcast benefit.
**Death Ward** — No upcast benefit.
**Dimension Door** — No upcast benefit.
**Divination** — No upcast benefit.
**Dominate Beast** — Concentration duration: 5th-level slot = up to 10 minutes; 6th-level slot = up to 1 hour; 7th-level slot or higher = up to 8 hours.
**Elemental Bane** — When cast with a slot of 5th level or higher, target one additional creature for each slot level above 4th.
**Evard's Black Tentacles** — No upcast benefit.
**Fabricate** — No upcast benefit.
**Find Greater Steed** — No upcast benefit.
**Fire Shield** — No upcast benefit.
**Freedom of Movement** — No upcast benefit.
**Galder's Speedy Courier** — When cast with an 8th-level slot, you can send the chest to a creature on a different plane of existence from you.
**Gate Seal** — When cast with a slot of 6th level or higher, the spell lasts until dispelled.
**Giant Insect** — No upcast benefit.
**Grasping Vine** — No upcast benefit.
**Gravity Sinkhole** — Damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 4th.
**Greater Invisibility** — No upcast benefit.
**Guardian of Faith** — No upcast benefit.
**Guardian of Nature** — No upcast benefit.
**Hallucinatory Terrain** — No upcast benefit.
**Ice Storm** — Bludgeoning damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 4th.
**Leomund's Secret Chest** — No upcast benefit.
**Locate Creature** — No upcast benefit.
**Mordenkainen's Faithful Hound** — No upcast benefit.
**Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum** — Cube size increases by 100 feet (per side) for each slot level beyond 4th.
**Otiluke's Resilient Sphere** — No upcast benefit.
**Phantasmal Killer** — Damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 4th.
**Polymorph** — No upcast benefit.
**Raulothim's Psychic Lance** — Damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 4th.
**Shadow Of Moil** — No upcast benefit.
**Sickening Radiance** — No upcast benefit.
**Spirit Of Death** — When cast with a slot of 5th level or higher, use the higher level wherever the spell's level appears in the reaper spirit stat block.
**Staggering Smite** — No upcast benefit.
**Stone Shape** — No upcast benefit.
**Stoneskin** — No upcast benefit.
**Storm Sphere** — Damage for each of its effects increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 4th.
**Summon Aberration** — When cast with a higher-level slot, use the higher level wherever the spell's level appears in the stat block.
**Summon Construct** — When cast with a higher-level slot, use the higher level wherever the spell's level appears in the stat block.
**Summon Elemental** — When cast with a slot of 5th level or higher, use the higher level wherever the spell's level appears in the stat block (affects AC, HP, Slam damage, multiattack).
**Summon Greater Demon** — The challenge rating of the demon you can summon increases by 1 for each slot level above 4th.
**Vitriolic Sphere** — Initial damage increases by 2d4 for each slot level above 4th.
**Wall of Fire** — Damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 4th.
**Watery Sphere** — No upcast benefit.
