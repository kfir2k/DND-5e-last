# 3rd-Level Spells — "At Higher Levels" Upcast Facts

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:<kebab-case-name> (one page per spell, individually
fetched and verified). Fetched: 2026-09-22.

Each line is the verbatim (or near-verbatim, where the page phrases it without a labeled
paragraph) numeric upcast scaling for that spell, or "No upcast benefit" if the spell's own
wikidot page has no "At Higher Levels" section. This file records only the numeric mechanical
fact for casting the spell with a slot above its base level (3rd) — not the spell's full rules
text or flavor text.

**Animate Dead** — When cast with a slot of 4th level or higher, you animate/control two
additional undead creatures for each slot level above 3rd (each from a different corpse or pile
of bones).

**Antagonize** — Damage increases by 1d4 for each slot level above 3rd.

**Ashardalon's Stride** — Speed increases by 5 feet and fire damage increases by 1d6, each for
every slot level above 3rd.

**Aura of Vitality** — No upcast benefit.

**Beacon of Hope** — No upcast benefit.

**Bestow Curse** — Duration scales with slot level: 4th level slot = concentration, up to 10
minutes; 5th level slot = 8 hours (no concentration required); 7th level slot = 24 hours;
9th-level slot = until dispelled.

**Blinding Smite** — No upcast benefit.

**Blink** — No upcast benefit.

**Call Lightning** — Damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 3rd.

**Catnap** — No upcast benefit.

**Clairvoyance** — No upcast benefit.

**Conjure Animals** — Casting with a higher-level slot summons more creatures of the chosen
option: twice as many with a 5th-level slot, three times as many with a 7th-level slot, four
times as many with a 9th-level slot.

**Conjure Barrage** — No upcast benefit.

**Counterspell** — When cast with a slot of 4th level or higher, the interrupted spell
automatically fails if its level is less than or equal to the level of the slot used.

**Create Food and Water** — No upcast benefit.

**Crusader's Mantle** — No upcast benefit.

**Daylight** — No upcast benefit.

**Dispel Magic** — When cast with a slot of 4th level or higher, automatically ends the effects
of a spell on the target if that spell's level is equal to or less than the slot used.

**Elemental Weapon** — With a 5th or 6th level slot: attack bonus +2, extra damage 2d4. With a
7th level slot or higher: attack bonus +3, extra damage 3d4.

**Enemies Abound** — No upcast benefit.

**Erupting Earth** — Damage increases by 1d12 for each slot level above 3rd.

**Fast Friends** — Can target one additional creature for each slot level above 3rd (targets
must be within 30 feet of each other).

**Fear** — No upcast benefit.

**Feign Death** — No upcast benefit.

**Fireball** — Damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd.

**Flame Arrows** — Number of pieces of ammunition affected increases by two for each slot level
above 3rd.

**Fly** — Can target one additional creature for each slot level above 3rd.

**Galder's Tower** — The tower gains one additional story for each slot level above 3rd.

**Gaseous Form** — No upcast benefit.

**Glyph of Warding** — An explosive runes glyph's damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level
above 3rd. (A spell glyph can store any spell up to the same level as the slot used to create
the glyph — this scales with the slot used to cast Glyph of Warding itself, not a separate
upcast bonus.)

**Haste** — No upcast benefit.

**Hunger of Hadar** — No upcast benefit.

**Hypnotic Pattern** — No upcast benefit.

**Incite Greed** — No upcast benefit.

**Intellect Fortress** — Can target one additional creature for each slot level above 3rd
(targets must be within 30 feet of each other when targeted).

**Leomund's Tiny Hut** — No upcast benefit.

**Life Transference** — Damage (to the caster, transferred as healing) increases by 1d8 for
each slot level above 3rd.

**Lightning Arrow** — Damage for both of the spell's damage effects increases by 1d8 for each
slot level above 3rd.

**Lightning Bolt** — Damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd.

**Magic Circle** — Duration increases by 1 hour for each slot level above 3rd.

**Major Image** — When cast with a slot of 6th level or higher, the illusion lasts until
dispelled, without requiring concentration (sometimes called "Permanent Image").

**Mass Healing Word** — Healing increases by 1d4 for each slot level above 3rd.

**Meld into Stone** — No upcast benefit.

**Melf's Minute Meteors** — The number of meteors created increases by two for each slot level
above 3rd.

**Motivational Speech** — Temporary hit points increase by 5 for each slot level above 3rd.

**Nondetection** — No upcast benefit.

**Phantom Steed** — No upcast benefit.

**Plant Growth** — No upcast benefit.

**Protection from Energy** — No upcast benefit.

**Pulse Wave** — Damage increases by 1d6 and the distance creatures are pushed/pulled increases
by 5 feet, each for every slot level above 3rd.

**Remove Curse** — No upcast benefit.

**Revivify** — No upcast benefit.

**Sending** — No upcast benefit.

**Sleet Storm** — No upcast benefit.

**Slow** — No upcast benefit.

**Speak with Dead** — No upcast benefit.

**Speak with Plants** — No upcast benefit.

**Spirit Guardians** — Damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 3rd.

**Spirit Shroud** — Damage increases by 1d8 for every two slot levels above 3rd.

**Stinking Cloud** — No upcast benefit.

**Summon Fey** — When cast with a slot of 4th level or higher, use the higher slot level
wherever the spell's level appears in the summoned creature's stat block.

**Summon Lesser Demons** — With a 6th or 7th level slot: summon twice as many demons. With an
8th or 9th level slot: summon three times as many demons.

**Summon Shadowspawn** — When cast with a slot of 4th level or higher, use the higher slot
level wherever the spell's level appears in the summoned creature's stat block.

**Summon Undead** — When cast with a slot of 4th level or higher, use the higher slot level
wherever the spell's level appears in the summoned creature's stat block.

**Thunder Step** — Damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 3rd.

**Tidal Wave** — No upcast benefit.

**Tiny Servant** — Can animate two additional objects for each slot level above 3rd.

**Tongues** — No upcast benefit.

**Vampiric Touch** — Damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 3rd.

**Wall of Sand** — No upcast benefit.

**Wall of Water** — No upcast benefit.

**Water Breathing** — No upcast benefit.

**Water Walk** — No upcast benefit.

**Wind Wall** — No upcast benefit.
