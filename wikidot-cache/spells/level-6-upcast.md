# 6th-level spells — "At Higher Levels" upcast notes

Source: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:<slug> (individual spell pages), fetched 2026-09-22.
Numeric scaling only, extracted verbatim from each spell's "At Higher Levels" section where present.

**Arcane Gate** — No upcast benefit.
**Blade Barrier** — No upcast benefit.
**Bones of the Earth** — When cast with a slot of 7th level or higher, create two additional pillars for each slot level above 6th.
**Chain Lightning** — One additional bolt leaps from the first target to another target for each slot level above 6th.
**Circle of Death** — Damage increases by 2d6 for each slot level above 6th.
**Contingency** — No upcast benefit.
**Create Homunculus** — No upcast benefit.
**Create Undead** — 7th-level slot: 4 ghouls. 8th-level slot: 5 ghouls OR 2 ghasts/wights. 9th-level slot: 6 ghouls OR 3 ghasts/wights OR 2 mummies.
**Disintegrate** — Damage increases by 3d6 for each slot level above 6th.
**Drawmij's Instant Summons** — No upcast benefit.
**Druid Grove** — No upcast benefit.
**Eyebite** — No upcast benefit.
**Find the Path** — No upcast benefit.
**Fizban's Platinum Shield** — No upcast benefit.
**Flesh to Stone** — No upcast benefit.
**Forbiddance** — No upcast benefit.
**Globe of Invulnerability** — When cast with a slot of 7th level or higher, the barrier blocks spells of one level higher for each slot level above 6th.
**Gravity Fissure** — Damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 6th.
**Guards and Wards** — No upcast benefit.
**Harm** — No upcast benefit.
**Heal** — Amount of healing increases by 10 for each slot level above 6th.
**Heroes' Feast** — No upcast benefit.
**Investiture of Flame** — No upcast benefit.
**Investiture of Ice** — No upcast benefit.
**Investiture of Stone** — No upcast benefit.
**Investiture of Wind** — No upcast benefit.
**Magic Jar** — No upcast benefit.
**Mass Suggestion** — 7th-level slot: duration becomes 10 days. 8th-level slot: 30 days. 9th-level slot: a year and a day.
**Mental Prison** — No upcast benefit.
**Move Earth** — No upcast benefit.
**Otiluke's Freezing Sphere** — Damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 6th.
**Otto's Irresistible Dance** — No upcast benefit.
**Planar Ally** — No upcast benefit.
**Primordial Ward** — No upcast benefit.
**Programmed Illusion** — No upcast benefit.
**Scatter** — No upcast benefit.
**Soul Cage** — No upcast benefit.
**Summon Fiend** — The fiendish spirit gains +15 hit points for each spell level above 6th.
**Sunbeam** — No upcast benefit.
**Tasha's Otherworldly Guise** — No upcast benefit.
**Tenser's Transformation** — No upcast benefit.
**Transport via Plants** — No upcast benefit.
**True Seeing** — No upcast benefit.
**Wall of Ice** — Damage when the wall appears increases by 2d6 for each slot level above 6th; damage from frigid air increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 6th.
**Wall of Thorns** — Both damage types (entering/starting-turn) increase by 1d8 for each slot level above 6th.
**Wind Walk** — No upcast benefit.
